## Combat continuation

For entry spell slot 131 and runtime group 1, the three group comparisons and nineteen
spell-ID comparisons skip their siblings and reach 0x5CBBCA. The route executes no calls
or memory writes: its six checking-call sites are skipped for the valid slot/product.
Thus the repeated group reads remain stable along this bounded route.

The [existing combat tail](F250.2.combat.reconstruction.md) then calls its script bridge at
0x5CBBCD and `RecalculateUnits(True,-1,-1,-1)` at 0x5CBBDC. The
[recalculation assessment](F250.2.recalculation-assessment.md) owns the callee scope and
calculator-equivalence analysis. No sibling body or full enclosing admission is inferred.

## Overland continuation and existing record join

The guard reads target displacement 0x642696C and then tests local `w != 0`. If both pass,
three direct writes follow, each reloading/checking the target: byte 0 at 0x5C4DE9
(displacement 0x6426D94), dword −1 at 0x5C4E19 (0x6426D6C), dword −1 at 0x5C4E4C
(0x6426D70). The [known calculated-record base](F264.1.evidence.md#record-base-provenance)
0x6426898 places these writes in `Units`, at offsets 0x4FC, 0x4D4 and 0x4D8. They are not
additional base-enchantment stores or proven retained state after the following base copy.

Spell tests 229 and 168 are explicit external exits. They are skipped while the slot remains
131; returning checking helpers can change that state, so the source retains both boundaries.
At 0x5C5032 the direct setup is EAX=0, EDX from target displacement 0x1AC1C74, earlier ECX
from 0x1AC1C44 and an earlier push from 0x1AC1C48. Target values are reloaded separately;
returning checking helpers may change earlier registers or stack contents.

Existing `BaseUnits` base 0x1AC1798 and the declaration in
[RecalculateUnits](Units.RecalculateUnits.pas) identify those three fields as plane (+0x4DC),
overlandx (+0x4AC) and overlandy (+0x4B0). On paths without intervening helper changes,
the call is `RecalculateUnits(False, baseTarget.plane, baseTarget.overlandx, baseTarget.overlandy)`.
The callee's two unit loops skip dead base units; with `com=False`, noncombat units remain
eligible. When X is not −1, both loops filter to that plane/X/Y; X=−1 disables location
filtering. The call therefore normally processes the target's tile, not just its one record.
The indexing global is the same one exported as `SPELLTARGETUNIT` in the tail.

## Complete overland tail

After recalculation the caller jumps to 0x5C84B5. Eight `SetScriptNumVar` calls export
SP, W, SPELLTARGETCITY/PLANE/X/Y/UNIT/PLAYER, then 0x5C856C calls `RunScript` using an
indirect runtime selector and DL=1. No script filename or transitive effects are inferred.

Two post-script signed cost gates remain explicit: ≥370 may queue/log a trigger; ≥301
may run the per-wizard candidate loop, including `NewUnit` and `checkover9regroup` calls.
The configured True Sight costs (15 and 12) are below both thresholds, but the packet does
not establish the local's value after script/helper calls. The full blocks therefore remain
in the source. The candidate retry has no explicit lower-bound exit or termination guarantee.

The final direct call sequence is `Checkfordrowning` (0x5C89F5),
`RefreshUnitsOnMapStatus` with AL=1 (0x5C89FC), `Recalculatevisibility` (0x5C8A01), and
`ResetAllCityData` (0x5C8A06), followed by exception-frame unlink, six string cleanup calls
and return. No second direct call to 0x599920 occurs in this new packet. Every excluded
callee can have further effects; this is not proof of exactly one transitive recalculation.

Both independent derivations, reciprocal reviews in their original sessions and fresh
revisions completed successfully using Astra medium and Claude Fable 5.1 medium. Initial
sessions: GPT `01a08fb6-3423-7e60-aa1e-4c7fb35adb65`, Claude
`78e0492d-d55b-485a-bb9d-036414b205ec`; fresh sessions:

The merge adopts their agreed control/writes and GPT's explicit live-state notation.
It omits Claude's stack-balance inference and source indexing that can conceal a changed
register after a checking helper. Unknown helper behavior and post-script cost values are
explicit limits, not reasons to reconstruct unrelated callees. Existing layout/signature
joins above were made after the independent rounds. Original answers remain unchanged in
`.derivations/F250.2.truesight-continuation.{GPT,Claude}{,.revised}.md`; reciprocal reviews
and the coordinator report are under `.reviews/F250.2-truesight-continuation*`.
