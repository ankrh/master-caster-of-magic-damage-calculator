# F250.2 — modern cast recalculation assessment

Existing-source reconciliation only. The compiled binary is shared by CoM2 1.05.11 and
Warlord 1.5.12.9; versioned runtime records and scripts remain separate inputs. No new
reconstruction or calculator behavior change.

## Known call and its scope

The [combat reconstruction](F250.2.combat.reconstruction.md) reaches `CommonTail_5CBBCA`
on the paths listed below. It calls the spell script bridge at `0x5CBBCD`, then directly
calls `0x599920` at `0x5CBBDC` with `AL=1`, `EDX=-1`, `ECX=-1` and a pushed `-1`.
The existing [RecalculateUnits](Units.RecalculateUnits.pas) declaration and body bind this
to `RecalculateUnits(True, -1, -1, -1)`; no wrapper or new callee reconstruction is needed.

The callee filters out dead and noncombat base units. The negative X argument disables
location filtering. Its scope is therefore the eligible combat units, not just the spell
target. For each eligible unit it copies `BaseUnits` into `Units`, aggregates enchantment
layers, runs the configured early hook, compiled calculation, configured late hook and
clamps. After the first unit loop it calls `BuildAuraTable` at `0x5A6822`, then runs the
second loop for auras and post-aura effects. This is the full engine combat recalculation.
The caller then has conditional movement-helper calls; those are outside this assessment.

## What this permits in the calculator design

For the established curse continuations, calling a shared full **faithful engine-model** combat
pass at the corresponding point follows the source. That does not yet prove equivalence to
the calculator's current `deriveUnitStats` chain. The replacement must preserve the script
bridge before recalculation, eligible-unit/aura scope, each hook's position, persistent writes
and the engine's copy/reset behavior. Recalculation must not replay attempted casts.

Concrete persistent-write witnesses already exist in `RecalculateUnits`: Blood Lust at
`0x59F5DC..0x59F747` and Animated at `0x59F7D8..0x59FBD0` write the base Undead flag as
well as calculated state. These show why adding or moving a full pass needs a check; they
are not by themselves a demonstrated counterexample for every spell or every input.

Therefore:

- **Known:** several scoped modern cast continuations already request the full combat pass.
- **Unresolved:** equivalence of an unconditional full calculator pass after every attempted
  effect, particularly rejected casts, the unsupplied True Sight continuations and overland
  script casts. No new numerical claim or provenance promotion follows from this document.

F279.2 owns representation and full-pass equivalence. Existing census slices P4.1, P4.3,
P4.4, P4.10 and P4.11 retain their per-effect context work; this assessment does not approve
those proposed slices or enlarge F250.2 into full lifecycle reconstruction.
