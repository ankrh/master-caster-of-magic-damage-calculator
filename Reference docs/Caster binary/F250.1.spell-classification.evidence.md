## NonMagic default and its limit

C2:338–339 and W:620–621 document:

> NonMagic - The spell is not blocked by Magic Immunity. (by default, spells are blocked by Magic Immunity)

The following line also describes exemption from defenses against magic. The paragraph sits under **Damage dealing spells can use the following parameters** (C2:330, W:612). Every one of the 15 scoped record occurrences omits NonMagic, but the legend does not prove loader defaults or enforcement by every curse/buff handler. In particular, missing NonMagic on True Sight/Rebuild does not establish that Magic Immunity rejects beneficial buffs. An absent record has no NonMagic classification at all.

[F250.4](F250.4.script-reconciliation.md) identifies Warlord Conjuring Pact [343] as the backing spell for the calculator's synthetic `nausea` input; its explicit NonMagic=True is separate from this name-search result.
