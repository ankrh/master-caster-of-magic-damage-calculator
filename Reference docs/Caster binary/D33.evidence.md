# D33 — `@Units@ApplyMagicWeapons` reconstruction evidence

## Scope and provenance

D33 reconstructs the complete half-open extent `$00598D88..$005992CC` (1,348 bytes / 348
instructions) in the CoM2 1.05.11 engine. Warlord 1.5.12.9 bundles a byte-identical engine copy;
the executable identity remains owned solely by [`README.md`](./README.md). Calculator versions
in scope are `com2_1.05.11` and `com2_warlord_1.5.12.9`.

Before D33, [D29](./D29.evidence.md) owned only the routine boundary, callers, exits and fixed
reconstruction frontier. D33 adds the source-shaped body to
[`Units.RecalculateUnits.pas`](./Units.RecalculateUnits.pas). It is evidence-only and changes no
calculator behavior.

Method 4 was used: two independent Codex GPT-5.6 Sol High derivations covered the complete package;
the merged Codex result passed the mechanical checker; one Claude Opus 5 High review independently
re-derived the bytes and accepted the reconstruction with binding/documentation refinements. The
review found no byte-level semantic error. Its accepted refinements—`Maxwizards`, the zero-ranged-
type consequence, the neighbouring literal-versus-INI To-Hit difference, relocated-pointer
wording, exact statement order, checked SmallInt rendering and second-caller ABI—are incorporated
here and in the source-shaped body.

## Reconstructed behavior

The routine returns without a gameplay write unless the persistent/base unit has `EncMagic`,
`EncMithril` or `EncAdamant`. When any material is present:

1. It tentatively grants calculated `EncMagic`, then scans configured player slots
   `0..GameDataType.Maxwizards`. A King of the Underworld belonging to any player other than the
   unit's current signed owner suppresses only that calculated assignment. The scan has no early
   exit; the owner's own global is ignored.
2. Material strength starts at 0 for Magic, becomes 1 for Mithril and becomes 2 for Adamantium.
   The two higher-material tests are sequential, so Adamantium wins if both flags coexist.
3. Positive persistent/base melee Attack receives the runtime To-Hit value, material strength and
   the same signed display-bonus increment, in that order.
4. Defense and its signed display bonus always receive material strength inside the material body.
5. A current ranged type that is not magical receives ranged strength, its signed display bonus
   and runtime To-Hit. `Ismagicalranged(0)` is False, so this also writes to a unit with no ranged
   attack; there is no ranged-strength-positive gate.
6. Positive current/calculated Thrown receives runtime To-Hit and then material strength. There is
   no Thrown display-bonus write.

All destinations are current/calculated `Units`. The six persistent `BaseUnits` reads are the
three initial material tests, the later Mithril and Adamantium tier tests, and the melee Attack
gate. The routine performs no persistent write.

Executable and executing-script tables agree on `EncMagic=1`, `EncMithril=2`, `EncAdamant=3`
and `GEKingOfUnderworld=30`. Literal assignments at `$00598EA2..$00598EA4`, `$00598F07` and
`$00598F3C` bind material strengths 0, 1 and 2.

The pointer slot `$0070A070` has a relocated static initializer that points to the runtime Integer
storage. Exactly four directly addressed code operands reference the slot: three consumers at
`$00598F99`, `$00599215`, `$0059927D`, and the pointee producer at `$006338A4`. The producer lies
in `@Init@GameInitialize` `$00633890..$006338AA`, reads `[Gameplay] MagicWeaponBonusHit` with
default 10, and writes the result through the pointer. No directly addressed instruction writes
the slot itself. Both scoped `MODDING.INI` files assign 10.

The immediately preceding region-`c` non-material magical-striker path adds literal 10 instead of
reading this runtime value. The two paths therefore agree in the shipped configurations but diverge
if `MagicWeaponBonusHit` is modded.

| `$00599257 7E6F JLE` | current Thrown nonpositive | `$005992C8`, epilogue |

The only semantic call is `$00599136 E8B9D2FFFF` to the already reconstructed
`@Units@Ismagicalranged` at `$005963F4`. The other 61 calls are 27 `@System@@BoundErr` and 34
`@System@@IntOver` compiler guards. The sole return is `$005992CB C3`.

The eleven gameplay writes occur in this exact order:

1. `$00598ED1` calculated `EncMagic := True` when admitted.
2. `$00598FA1` melee To-Hit.
3. `$00598FD6` Attack strength.
4. `$00599055` signed Attack display bonus.
5. `$00599084` Defense strength.
6. `$00599103` signed Defense display bonus.
7. `$0059916A` ranged strength.
8. `$005991E9` signed ranged display bonus.
9. `$0059921D` ranged To-Hit.
10. `$00599285` Thrown To-Hit.
11. `$005992BA` Thrown strength.

All seven 32-bit additions use checked `ADD/JNO`, otherwise `@System@@IntOver`. Each of the three
SmallInt display assignments uses the complete signed checked-conversion idiom: `MOVSX`, checked
addition, `+$8000`, unsigned `$FFFF` range guard with `@System@@BoundErr`, `+$FFFF8000`, then word
store. The complete ranges are `$00599008..$00599055`, `$005990B6..$00599103` and
`$0059919C..$005991E9`.

## Edges and Warlord composition

Exactly two direct callers target the entry and none targets the interior:

| Call | Caller and argument |
|---|---|
| `$0059E4B0` | `@Units@RecalculateUnits`, EAX = current unit index `i` from `$0059E4AD` |
| `$00649299` | `@Castercore@CreateMercenaryPreview`, EAX = preview slot `Maxunits + 1`; `$00649262` first sets that slot's base `EncMagic` |

Warlord's early `UnitCalcPre.CAS` hook precedes this helper; its only scoped material-token match
is a calculated `EncMagic` write. The later `UnitCalc.CAS` hook at `$005A65D7` follows the helper
and precedes final clamps at `$005A65DC`. It contains none of `EncMagic`, `EncMithril`,
`EncAdamant`, or `MagicWeaponBonusHit`, so it does not replace the compiled material gates or tiers;
its later overlapping stat writes compose after them.

## Dependency correction and bounded residuals

D33's review corrected D29's former reloc-stripped premise: this PE retains base relocations, and a
HIGHLOW entry covers `$0070A070`. D29's no-stored-code-pointer conclusion survives an exhaustive
scan; [D29 evidence](./D29.evidence.md) now records the corrected premise and result.

The review also noted three bounded external questions without changing D33: whether both writers
of `Maxwizards` always clamp to 13; whether the layout tool's reported WizardT `+8` reflects
GameData alignment rather than record drift; and the generic residual possibility of a computed
pointer write to the `$0070A070` page. None leaves a byte, branch, call, state write or applicable
runtime constant inside D33 unresolved.
