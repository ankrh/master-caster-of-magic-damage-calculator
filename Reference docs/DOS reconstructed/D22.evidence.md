# D22 evidence — Fireball `SPELLDAT.LBX` record

## Scope and identity

D22 covers Fireball spell ID 96 in MoM 1.31 and MoM CP 1.60: the complete 36-byte
`SPELLDAT.LBX` record `[0x0FA4,0x0FC8)`. Calculator verification scope is `mom_1.31` and
`mom_cp_1.60.00`; no calculator behavior changed.

The executable and table paths, sizes, and hashes are owned by
[MoM binary analysis.md](../MoM%20binary%20analysis.md), *The binaries*.

Both LBX files declare one entry `[0x0220,0x2060)`. Its subheader at `0x0220` is
`D7 00 24 00`: 215 records of `0x24` bytes beginning at `0x0224`. Therefore:

```text
0x0224 + 215 * 0x24 = 0x2060
0x0224 +  96 * 0x24 = 0x0FA4
```

The record begins with the NUL-padded name `Fireball`, independently confirming the index.

## Inherited Wall of Fire strength

The executable side of the binding was already reconstructed and remains owned by:

- [R6.2e.evidence.md](./R6.2e.evidence.md), `Check_Wall_Of_Fire_Attack`: MoM 1.31 and CP 1.60
  pass Fireball ID `0x60` with strength override zero at `0x9EE60..0x9EE6C`.
- [R6.2f.evidence.md](./R6.2f.evidence.md), `Apply_Battle_Unit_Damage_From_Spell`: override
  zero takes `0x8717E -> 0x87185`; `0x87193` (`26 8A 47 20`) loads record byte `+0x20`, and
  `0x87197` (`B4 00`) zero-extends it as attack strength.

The selected local can be decremented per attack only by the Warp Lightning guard at
`0x8733E..0x87345`. Fireball's attributes are `ATT_AREA` (`0x1000`), not
`ATT_WARP_LIGHTNING` (`0x8000`), so the inherited value remains 5 across iterations.

The byte at each record's raw file offset `0x0FC4` is `0x05`. Wall of Fire therefore inherits
unsigned base attack strength **5** in both `mom_1.31` and `mom_cp_1.60.00`.

## CP-only incoming Wall edge

Review of the merged derivation corrected one independent-track disagreement. CP 1.60 has a
live near call at `0x8AF6B` (`E8 DA E4`) to helper `[0x89448,0x89454)`, whose `0x89449`
instruction is `9A 43 00 E0 03`, a third call to `Check_Wall_Of_Fire_Attack`. MoM 1.31 has
the helper's comparison inline at `0x8AF6B` and no corresponding Wall call. The Wall body and
Fireball record are byte-identical, so this call-graph difference does not affect the strength-5
finding. [R6.version-differences.md](./R6.version-differences.md) owns the cross-build edge;
the containing trigger semantics are outside D22's extent and are reconstructed in
[D28.evidence.md](./D28.evidence.md).
