# R6.2b evidence — `BU_ProcessAttack`, first half

This is the durable evidence companion to [`combat.c`](combat.c). Claude and Codex derived the
extent independently, reciprocally reviewed the two derivations, and applied every accepted
correction. Claude withdrew its sole remaining objection after independently confirming the
`e_SPELL_BOOK_REALM` encoding; Codex merged the union on 2026-08-07. No disagreement survived
review.

## Scope and frame

| Build | Assigned executable extent | Routine entry / continuation | Frame |
|---|---|---|---|
| `mom131` | `[0x999C9, 0x99ED7)` | entry `0x999C9`; R6.2c continues at `0x99ED7` | `sub sp,0x2E`; save SI/DI |
| `mom160` | `[0x999C9, 0x99ED7)` | same | `sub sp,0x2E`; save SI/DI |
| `com1` | `[0x999C9, 0x99ED7)` | same | `sub sp,0x34`; save SI/DI |

The routine is `void`: only the `Figs <= 0` exit clears AX, while the flying refusal,
zero-strength exit, and main continuation leave unrelated values in AX; every known caller ignores
it. CoM's `bp-0x32` cache is a 16-bit battle-unit offset, reloaded into BX at `0x99D3D` while
ES remains the battle-unit segment. The source-shaped C represents that register pairing as a far
pointer without claiming a four-byte frame local.

## Merged findings

- The function clears the caller's three damage buckets at `0x99A7C`, aborts for
  `Figs <= 0` at `0x99A89..0x99A91`, combines common and channel attack attributes, and obtains
  the attack-immunity mask at `0x99AC2`.
- MoM 1.31 adds `ranged_tohit` only inside the mode-2 gate at
  `0x99B64..0x99B87`. CP 1.60 and CoM 1 hoist that addition so mode-1 thrown, breath, and gaze
  attacks receive it too. CoM heroes (`Hero_Slot > -1`) jump at `0x99B77` past the mode,
  Has-Ranged-Attack, and distance checks after receiving the bonus.
- A false Has-Ranged-Attack result forces strength zero. Magic-class ranged attacks skip distance
  loss; the other ranged attacks divide range by 3 in MoM/CP and by 4 in CoM at
  `0x99BB0..0x99BB6`, with Long Range clamping a positive penalty to 1. Lightning Breath 102 adds
  Armor Piercing; Multiple Gaze 104 adds Doom damage and CoM's marker bit.
- MoM 1.31 melee To Hit adds `melee_tohit - defender.toblock` at
  `0x99C21..0x99C41`. CP NOPs the subtraction but retains the now-dead To Block load; CoM adds
  only `melee_tohit`. CoM calls the frame-sharing helper at `0x99C00`, and Berserk from either
  battle or persistent enchantments doubles melee strength against races below 15 at
  `0x99C28..0x99C55`.
- In MoM/CP, defender Invisibility from persistent, battle, item, or ability sources subtracts one
  To Hit unless the attacker has Illusion immunity (`0x99C89..0x99CC4`). CoM replaces the
  complete 62-byte penalty block with NOPs (`0x99C89..0x99CC6`).
- A counterattack subtracts signed `Suppression / 2`, truncating toward zero
  (`0x99CC7..0x99CE4`). A nonflying attacker making its own melee attack against a flying
  defender returns unless it is counterattacking or has `ranged_type >= 100`
  (`0x99CE7..0x99D2F`; compact end `0x99D22` in CP/CoM).
- Defender Magic Immunity skips Immolation and both gaze-kill families. MoM 1.31 lets Immolation
  ride every attack mode; CP restricts it to melee; CoM restricts it to melee and raises strength
  from 4 to 10. CoM's non-melee arm performs a discarded Damage-Limit test and jumps over an
  unreachable spell-7 call whose strength would be attacker figures plus 2
  (`0x99D3D..0x99D73`).
- Stoning/Multiple Gaze performs one Nature resistance roll per current defender figure with
  modifier `-abs(Spec_Att_Attrib)`, adding failed figures' hits to damage bucket 2. Multiple/Death
  Gaze does the equivalent Death roll and adds to bucket 0 (`0x99D73..0x99EB0`). The realm
  constants are `e_SPELL_BOOK_REALM`: Nature 0, Chaos 2, Death 4. Black Sleep adds Doom damage;
  CoM also sets its marker.
- MoM 1.31 returns when attack strength is nonpositive at `0x99ECE..0x99ED4`. CP and CoM replace
  `7F 03` with `EB 03`, retaining the comparison but always continuing into R6.2c.
