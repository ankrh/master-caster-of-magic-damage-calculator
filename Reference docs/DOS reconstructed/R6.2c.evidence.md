# R6.2c evidence — `BU_ProcessAttack`, second half

This is the durable evidence companion to [`combat.c`](combat.c). Claude and Codex derived
all three `[0x99ED7,0x9A587)` extents independently and cross-reviewed them on 2026-08-07.
The merged source is in `combat.c`; this file holds coverage, inventories, arithmetic checks,
and findings.

The review disagreement concerned only the meaning of ledger `Within`; the bytes and CFG were
agreed. The README now defines it as the verifier's enclosing conditional-skip relation rather
than exclusive control-flow ownership. Accordingly, the CP/CoM city island retains `Within 9`
and the CoM two-caller helper retains `Within 36`, while their titles record alternate entries.

Verified: Claude 2026-08-07; Codex 2026-08-07, independent.

`[0x98F60, 0x99290)` and CoM 1's island `[0x9984B, 0x998BF)` was decoded linearly in each build
and every direct branch and call target collected. The only edges into `[0x99ED7, 0x9A587)` are:

`0x99ED7` is therefore the extent's only body entry, and `0x9A581` its only externally targeted
interior address. CoM 1's near helper at `0x9A256` is reached only from `0x9A218` and `0x9A267`,
both inside.

## Merged findings

1. Eldritch Weapon decrements the local defender To Block copy before both defence rolls
   (`0x99EEF..0x99EF8`); it does not alter the earlier attack To Hit calculation.
2. City defence is +3 on the positive coordinate query and +1 otherwise. CP 1.60 and CoM 1 add
   the out-of-line coordinate island at `0x99F7C..0x99F91`, entered from `0x99F31`.
3. Defender Magic Immunity skips Dispel Evil, Stoning Touch, Death Touch, Life Steal and
   Destruction as one group (`0x99F67..0x99F71`). Poison has its separate immunity gate.
4. MoM Dispel Evil accepts Chaos/Death races with base modifier -4 and a further -5 for Undead.
   CoM 1 accepts every signed race >= 0x0F, uses -3/-3, exempts Spell Lock, and retains an
   unreachable `race == 0x14` comparison (`0x99F96..0x99FC8`).
5. Channel flags alter Stoning Touch by -1 and Death Touch by -3. CoM 1's Life Steal path tests
   raw `0x0040` (Illusionary), subtracts 2 in live AX, and uses the resistance margin as both
   Undeath damage and the amount passed to opaque overlay `0370:002A`
   (`0x9A14C..0x9A19D`).
6. Destruction rolls once per attacking figure after the shared Magic-Immunity gate. MoM adds the
   defender's per-figure hits to irreversible damage; CoM 1 adds flat 100
   (`0x9A19E..0x9A1E5`).
7. Automatic/Doom damage bypasses attack roll, Blur, defence and Invulnerability. MoM halves it
   when the channel's `0x0010` flag is set; CoM 1's marker test is inverted, so marker bit 1
   selects full strength (`0x9A1E6..0x9A206`).
8. MoM 1.31 Blur tests the defender's Illusion Immunity and has the shrinking-bound loop; CP 1.60
   switches the immunity reads to the attacker and makes each original hit receive one 10% roll.
   CoM 1 uses attacker immunity, 20% for Blur or Invisibility, and 30% when stacked
   (`0x9A216..0x9A2A9`).
9. CoM's call at `0x9A213` accumulates the attack roll into `[bp-8]` in callee
   `0x9984B` and retains `sar(roll-5,1)`. The call at `0x9A2CA` may restore that retained
   floor at `0x99870` when defence is below 80 and Destruction is present. The callee bodies
   remain assigned to R6.2d.
10. Poison makes one realm-less resistance check per Poison strength. CoM 1 reuses -1 as both
    realm and modifier where MoM uses modifier 0 (`0x9A30F..0x9A386`). Later builds widen all
    Create Undead blocking masks from raw `0x0020` to `0x0060`.
11. The final split consumes whole defender figures while
    `hits <= attack_damage + accumulated_damage`, routes overkill/exact/residual damage to the
    three caller buckets, carries front-figure damage, and flushes rider buckets once per attacking
    figure (`0x9A387..0x9A580`).
