# R6.1h — item attack-special helper

Verified: Claude 2026-08-07, independent derivation; Codex 2026-08-07, independent derivation;
both reciprocal reviews completed 2026-08-07. Merged by Codex 2026-08-07. One source-rendering
disagreement survives as `disputed` below and is registered as Q25; the binary bytes, control
flow, names, values, and mechanic findings are not disputed. Executable identities are recorded
once in `../MoM binary analysis.md`, *The binaries*.

Assigned half-open extent, including the terminal return:

| Routine | mom131 | mom160 | com1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| `BU_Apply_Item_Attack_Specials` | `[0x8E4C4, 0x8E668)` | `=` | `=` |

All 420 bytes have SHA-256
`915d37c566ed90b2a044d608000e4f1bc73b74d881422b59a27f31cb41a11513` in each build.
`0x8E4C3: CB` terminates the preceding item-power helper; `0x8E4C4: 55 8B EC` is this
routine's prologue; `0x8E667: CB` is its `retf`; and
`0x8E668: 55 8B EC 83 EC 04` begins the constructor hit-point helper. Linear 16-bit decode
consumes 420 of 420 bytes as 164 complete instructions.

The item record is `0x32` bytes. Every block reloads the far `_ITEMS` base from DS:`0x9136`,
then reads `Powers` as its low word at record `+0x2E` and high word at `+0x30`.

## Calls and state writes

The helper makes zero calls. Its two byte-identical callers use Borland's `90 0E E8 rel16`
same-segment far-call idiom and remove three argument words with `83 C4 06`:

| Selected destination | Address evidence |
|---|---|
| `bu->melee_attack_attributes` (`+0x28`) | `0x8DE4B: 05 28 00`; call `0x8DE54: E8 6D 06 -> 0x8E4C4` |
| `bu->ranged_attack_attributes` (`+0x2A`) | `0x8DFFB: 05 2A 00`; call `0x8E004: E8 BD 04 -> 0x8E4C4` |

The helper reads the selected word once at `0x8E4CE` and writes it once at
`0x8E662: 26 89 0F` after nine conditional register-accumulator updates. It writes no item,
global, or other battle-unit state.

`verify_dos_derivation.py` reports 11 named-field writes because its syntactic detector mistakes
the initial selected-word read and nine `Powers` high-word reads for destinations. The one real
memory destination is `0x8E662`; all eleven checker candidates are cited.

## Findings

### Nine item powers map directly to nine attack-attribute flags

The routine reloads `_ITEMS[item].Powers` for every test and ORs the corresponding flag into the
caller-selected attack-attribute word. The executable's own name→mask table at `0x2D0B0`
supplies the output names through `0x0800`.

| Item-power input | Input mask / test | Attack-attribute output | Output mask / OR |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vampiric | `0x00000001`; `0x8E4E6` | Life Steal | `0x0008`; `0x8E4F8` |
| Lightning | `0x00000004`; `0x8E512` | Armor Piercing | `0x0001`; `0x8E524` |
| Destruction | `0x00000010`; `0x8E53E` | Destruction | `0x0020`; `0x8E550` |
| Chaos | `0x00000800`; `0x8E56A` | Automatic Damage | `0x0010`; `0x8E57D` |
| Death | `0x00010000`; `0x8E59A` | Death Touch | `0x0200`; `0x8E5A9` |
| Power Drain / CoM 1 Divine Protection slot | `0x01000000`; `0x8E5C6` | Power Drain | `0x0400`; `0x8E5D5` |
| Holy Avenger | `0x00200000`; `0x8E5F2` | Dispel Evil | `0x0800`; `0x8E601` |
| Phantasmal | `0x00800000`; `0x8E61E` | Illusionary | `0x0040`; `0x8E62D` |
| Stoning | `0x00001000`; `0x8E647` | Stoning Touch | `0x0080`; `0x8E65A` |

The helper itself has no weapon-type or attack-class gate. `BU_Apply_Hero_Items` supplies those
gates before calling it: melee at `0x8DE54`, ranged at `0x8E004`. Therefore one qualifying item
can add the mapped flag to melee, ranged, or both according to the caller paths it reaches.

### CoM 1's Divine Protection slot also sets the Power Drain attack flag

R6.1e established that CoM 1 repurposes raw item-power bit `0x01000000` as Divine Protection and
executes its Death Immunity/Lucky writes at `0x8DD49..0x8DD59`. This adjacent helper is
byte-identical across all three builds and independently tests that same raw bit at `0x8E5C6`,
then ORs attack-attribute `0x0400` at `0x8E5D5`. The executable's attack-attribute table names
`0x0400` Power Drain. CoM 1 therefore retains the old slot's attack-special side effect in
addition to the replacement defensive effects whenever the item's melee/ranged caller gate is
reached.

### The helper has no cross-build byte difference

MoM 1.31, CP 1.60, and CoM 1 execute the same 420 bytes at the same offsets. Version-specific
behavior arises from the meaning/data assigned to an input bit and the callers' live eligibility,
not from any change inside this helper.

  `#if BUILD == COM1 ... IP_COM1_DIVINE_PROTECTION ... #else ... IP_POWER_DRAIN` and states that

claiming a binary control-flow difference. Q25 retains the unresolved convention question; it
