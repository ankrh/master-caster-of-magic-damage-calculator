# D39 — reconstruct the CoM 6.08 standard attack resolver

Method 4 package `D39`. Evidence/build scope: CoM 6.08 `WIZARDS.EXE`; calculator scope: not
applicable. Two independent Codex GPT-5.6 Sol High derivations were merged, mechanically checked,
and reviewed once in the package's single Claude Opus 5 Medium session. The review accepted the
source-shaped reconstruction and found no durable source defect; six scratch-only wording findings
were incorporated before this evidence was written. Calculator behavior is unchanged.

## Result

`BU_ProcessAttack` is the complete far, caller-cleaned, void standard attack resolver
`[0x999C9,0x9A587)`. It receives attacker index, figure count, defender index, attack mode, a
caller-owned three-word output array, counterattack flag, and `SpFx`. Prologue
`55 8B EC 83 EC 34 56 57` begins at `0x999C9`; the sole far epilogue is
`5F 5E 8B E5 5D CB` at `0x9A581..0x9A586`. Every exit converges there.

The complete reconstruction confirms D38's central distinction and supplies the previously
missing surrounding behavior:

- executable-named Destruction is mask `$0020` and owns the live first-reduction damage floor;
- executable-named Supernatural is mask `$2000`, but its only resolver test is semantically dead;
- the floor is called exactly once per ordinary strike path and is not repeated after residual
  damage spills to another defender figure;
- the caller's three output words represent ordinary, create-undead, and irreversible damage;
- adjusted defense, both reduction sites, all composite-attribute injections, outer strike,
  poison, gaze, Blur, and inner figure/spill loops are source-shaped in `combat.c`.

No calculator formula was changed by D39. Its result removes the reconstruction dependency from
R9-G1g, which separately owns the calculator's direct `supernaturalMinimumDamage` decision.

## Direct mask names and decisive bytes

Attack-attribute table record `0x2D0D8` is `60 42 20 00 74 00 B2 01`. With data base
`0x294A0`, pointer `$4260` resolves to `0x2D700`, which spells `Destruction\0`; its mask is `$0020`.
Adjacent record `0x2D0D0` binds `$0010` to `Doom`. The source explicitly aliases that bit as
`ATT_DOOM_DAMAGE` and `ATT_AUTOMATIC_DAMAGE` because the resolver uses it as an automatic-damage
marker.

the test itself is `0x5C96C: 26 F6 47 1F 20`, byte mask `$20` in the high byte of common attack
attributes, hence word mask `$2000`. Reads at `0x5C56F/0x5C573` identify the pointed-to record as a
BATTLE_UNIT by its `+0x2C/+0x2E` item-enchantment pair, matching resolver reads

## Complete source-shaped flow

1. Cache defender front damage and combined base/battle/item enchantments; clear locals and the
   three output words; exit only after those output writes when `Figs <= 0`.
2. Start with common attack attributes and translate defense kind through `0x99150`. Ranged loads
   strength/type/realm, merges its channel, applies To Hit, the CoM hero exemption, availability,
   range/4 penalty, and Long Range cap. Melee calls `0x9AC1B`, applies melee To Hit, and doubles
   Berserk strength against a non-Fantastic defender.
3. Clear the rider buckets, apply attacker Suppression on counterattack, and reject an unaided
   nonflying melee attacker against a flying defender.
4. Under defender Magic Immunity, execute melee Immolation, retain the dead `$2000` test, and run
   per-defender-figure Stoning/Death gaze loops. Black Sleep injects Doom/Automatic Damage `$0010`.
5. Produce adjusted defense through `[0x9A587,0x9A79E)`, decrement To Block for Eldritch Weapon,
   and add the applicable fortress bonus. The fortress coordinate mapping is confirmed by
   initializer `0x75CA7`: BATTLE_UNIT `+0x44` is `cgx`, `+0x46` is `cgy`.
6. For every attacking figure, under the shared Magic-Immunity gate, resolve Dispel Evil, Stoning
   Touch, Death Touch, Life Steal, and Destruction. Destruction makes one Chaos resistance roll and
   adds flat 100 to irreversible bucket 2 on failure.
7. Doom/Automatic Damage bypasses ordinary attack, Blur, defense, and Invulnerability. Otherwise
   roll attack, save the floor candidate, apply the CoM 0/20/30% Invisibility/Blur loss unless the
   attacker has Illusion immunity, then perform first defense, Invulnerability `-2`, Destruction
   floor, and zero clamp.
8. Poison reads only the attacker's common Poison flag and makes one realm-less resistance check
   per Poison strength. Failed checks route through the Create Undead category predicate.
9. Add riders and carried front damage, enter the inner figure/spill loop, route whole and partial
   figures to ordinary or create-undead output, and apply fresh defense/Invulnerability/zero clamp
   to each conventional remainder without repeating the Destruction floor.
10. Route residual damage, update carried front damage, flush the three rider buckets into the
    caller array, clear them, and repeat through the requested attacking figures.

The parent supplies ranged/repeat-ranged, breath/gaze, retaliation, First Strike, ordinary melee,
and repeat-melee calls. Relocated tail `[0x99874,0x998A6)` is entered only by
`0x9ACFE: E9 73 EB`; normal parent control at `0x99849: EB 76` skips it and the trailing 27 NOPs
`[0x998A6,0x998C1)`.

## Review reconciliation

The independent review accepted `combat.c` without a source change. Its six findings were all in
the merged scratch prose and were incorporated before this durable record:

1. `55 8B EC 83 EC 34` is three instructions, not four.
2. Each parent call feeds one directional output array, not both; `0x99635` additionally feeds
   `Source_Unit_Damage`.
3. The apparent island is the relocated tail whose only live entry is `0x9ACFE`.
4. The UI record load is at `0x5C968`; the `$2000` test is at `0x5C96C`, and independent field
   reads identify the record as a BATTLE_UNIT.
5. Executable name Doom and source role Automatic Damage are explicit `$0010` aliases.
6. The far estimator saves its loop counter before pushing the seven actual argument words.

No substantive disagreement survives, so no backlog Q row is required.
