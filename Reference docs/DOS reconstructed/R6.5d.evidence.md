## Entry routing and embedded helpers

The primary entry jumps around both CoM helper islands and contains no call instruction.

- `0x9A8C8: EB 09` jumps over `0x9A8CA..0x9A8D2`. External caller

## Findings

1. **The target is the battlefield side-bonus aggregator.** It imports Cloud of Shadow into the
   defender Darkness slot in every build (`0x9A8B3..0x9A8C7`). MoM 1.31 and CP 1.60 also import
   Heavenly Light into defender True Light (`0x9A8C8..0x9A8DC`); CoM replaces those bytes with a
   jump around its To Block helper. It then scans every player for Eternal Night and, unless city
   True Light is already present, marks the attacker side, defender side, or both Darkness slots
   according to that player's combat-side identity (`0x9A8DD..0x9A936`).

2. **City defense changed from unconditional city-side +3 to fortified-city +2.** In MoM 1.31,
   mode 1 plus defender control adds 3 Defense (`0x9A98F..0x9A9CF`). CP and CoM require either
   `walled` or `wall_of_fire` and add 2 instead (`0x9A98F..0x9A9CF` CP;
   `0x9A98F..0x9A9CA` CoM). The CoM `BU_ApplyDamage` call at `0x8753C..0x87545` passes dseg
   `0xC520`, establishing that word as the combat-structure selector used by this gate.

3. **Holy Bonus and Resistance to All are strict per-controller maxima, then Holy Bonus folds
   into the Resist/Prayer array.** Active providers contribute signed `Spec_Att_Attrib` through
   `Attribs_2` bits `0x80` and `0x40` (`0x9A9E7..0x9AA8E`). Only a strictly greater provider
   replaces the current word. The final loop adds the Holy Bonus maximum to the shared
   Resist/Prayer maximum (`0x9ACD9..0x9ACFC`): MoM 1.31 iterates `num_players`, while CP and CoM
   iterate seven fixed side slots.

4. **Prayer and Leadership aggregation differs in all three builds.** MoM separately tests the
   four hero bits and uses signed `n`, `3n/2`, `n/3`, and `n/2`, where `n=Level+1`
   (`0x9AAF6..0x9ACCC`). CP zero-extends Level, combines each two-bit family, treats Prayer alone
   as `n` and every other nonzero Prayer combination as `n+n/2`, and treats Leadership alone as
   `n/3` and every other nonzero combination as logical `n/2` (`0x9AADA..0x9AB5D`). CoM returns
   to signed Level but performs two Prayer formulas with low-byte-only shifts, retains the common
   signed Leadership divisions, and adds five independent unknown SpFx maxima
   (`0x9AABA..0x9ACCC`).

5. **CP and CoM track which side changed the shared Resist/Prayer maximum.** They clear dseg
   `0xC7B4..0xC7B5` at `0x9A970`; successful Resistance or Prayer updates set slot 0 for the
   combat defender and slot 1 otherwise (`0x9AA73`, `0x9AB50` CP; `0x9AA77`, `0x9AB05`,
   `0x9ABF8` CoM). MoM has no such writes.

6. **CoM's embedded helpers are external entries, not primary-body calls.** The primary routine
   jumps over both islands, while `CMB_DefenseRoll` calls `0x9A8CA` and `BU_ProcessAttack` calls
   `0x9AC1B`. The second helper grants capped battle-unit mana from one of two raw hero-byte
   flags; its exact byte-wrap, signed multiply, logical shift, and unsigned cap are retained in
   `combat.c`. The primary routine itself makes zero calls in every build.

7. **The later binaries retain unreachable patch residue.** CP/CoM jump over an alternate
   Resist/Prayer store at `0x9AA82`/`0x9AA86`. CP also retains the superseded MoM hero tail at
   `0x9AB5F..0x9ACCF`; live CP flow reaches neither it nor its writes. These bytes remain
   reconstructed and inventoried rather than being mistaken for live behavior.
