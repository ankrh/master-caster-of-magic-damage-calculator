## Result

The frozen executable contains no executing binding from the binary-named Supernatural mask
`$2000` to the documented `max(0, floor((damage - 5) / 2))` minimum. The only exact arithmetic
candidate is BP-sharing helper `[0x9984B,0x99857)`, and its only application helper
`[0x9985B,0x99874)` gates on composite attack-attribute mask `$0020`. The executable independently
binds `$0020` to Destruction and `$2000` to Supernatural.

The standard attack resolver preserves common-field `$2000`, but its sole direct `$2000` test at
`0x99D58` is dead: `0x99D5D` is an unconditional jump and `0x99D73` replaces the flags before a
conditional branch. The natural routine containing that dead test and the live differently-gated
formula path is `[0x999C9,0x9A587)`, now owned by fixed-extent successor D39.

## High-byte/low-byte result

Byte ordering does not reconcile the two masks:

```asm
99861  F7 46 EC 20 00    test word [bp-0x14],0x0020
5C96C  26 F6 47 1F 20    test byte es:[bx+0x1F],0x20
```

The first instruction's little-endian word immediate `20 00` is `$0020`. In the second,
`+0x1F` is the high byte of the word at `+0x1E`, so byte mask `$20` is word mask `$2000`.
The resolver uses full-word loads and ORs, so no byte swap occurs:

```asm
99AA1  26 8B 47 1E       mov ax,es:[bx+0x1E]
99AA5  89 46 EC          mov [bp-0x14],ax
99AE3  26 0B 47 2A       or  ax,es:[bx+0x2A]
99BF9  26 0B 47 28       or  ax,es:[bx+0x28]
```

Within `[0x999C9,0x9A587)`, the complete set of direct masks tested against `[bp-0x14]` is
`$4000`, `$0800`, `$0080`, `$0200`, `$0008`, `$0020`, and `$0010`. `$2000` is never tested.

## Attribute provenance

Constructor `[0x8EAC4,0x8EAF2)` copies `0x24` bytes from the selected unit-type record to the
caller-supplied destination, including common attack attributes `+0x1E`. Adjacent writes prove the
destination is BATTLE_UNIT: `0x8EAF5` clears `+0x22` Combat_Effects, `0x8EB31` writes `+0x30`
unit index, and `0x8EB3A` clears `+0x64` Gold_Melee. Channel words `+0x28/+0x2A` are zeroed at
`0x8EB14/0x8EB1A`, so they are not copied unit-type data.

Resolver `[bp-0x14]` begins with common `+0x1E`, then ORs ranged `+0x2A` or melee `+0x28`.

## Live `$0020` arithmetic path

After the ordinary attack roll, the first BP-sharing helper accumulates raw successful hits and
computes the candidate:

```asm
9A20E  E8 4F ED          call 0x98F60
9A213  E8 35 F6          call 0x9984B

9984B  01 46 F8          add [bp-8],ax
9984E  2D 05 00          sub ax,5
99851  D1 F8             sar ax,1
99853  89 46 CC          mov [bp-0x34],ax
99856  C3                ret
```

For nonnegative raw hits `h`, `sar(h-5,1)` is signed `floor((h-5)/2)`. There is no Borland
truncate-toward-zero correction before the shift; the later zero clamp makes negative candidates
inert.

Adjacent defense routine `[0x9A587,0x9A79E)` returns ordinary Defense or full-block sentinel 100
at `0x9A78D`. The caller stores it in `[bp-0x0C]` at `0x99EEC`; `0x99F3D..0x99F52` then adds
either 3 or 1. The gate therefore compares an adjusted score against 80, corresponding to raw
threshold 77 or 79. Full block becomes 103 or 101 and still bypasses the minimum.

The first ordinary reduction applies the candidate only for adjusted defense below 80 and
composite `$0020`:


This makes `0x9A2CD` a first-path per-strike zero clamp, not the resolver's final output clamp.
A second reduction site `[0x9A499,0x9A4CE)` inside the figure loop repeats defense, item reduction,
and zero clamp but never calls `0x9985B`. Output is later accumulated into the caller-supplied
three-word `[bp+0x0E]` array. Complete per-figure/output semantics remain D39 work. D38 proves only
that the first reduction's minimum is live for `$0020` and not `$2000`.
