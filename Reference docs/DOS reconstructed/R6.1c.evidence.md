# R6.1c — battle-unit stat recompute, first half

The merged source-shaped reconstruction is in `unitcalc.c`. This item covers
`BU_Apply_Battlefield_Effects` over the half-open extents `mom131`/`mom160`
`0x8FF09..0x90635` and `com1` `0x8FF09..0x9064B`.

Both agents derived the item independently and reviewed the other's artifact. Every byte-backed
review entry was resolved before merge. The only temporary disagreement—whether CoM 1 wizard
byte `+0x6A` retained MoM's `divine_power` name—closed as **Guardian** after the CoM 1 retort
renumbering, helptext, gate and stat tuple were checked together. No disputed row remains.

## Findings

### MoM 1.31 and CP 1.60 are identical in this half

The two 1,836-byte slices are byte-identical. CP 1.60's first recompute differences occur later
in R6.1d (including Vertigo and Weakness), so every finding here that applies to MoM's first half
also applies to CP 1.60 at the same addresses.

### Node aura and Leadership have asymmetric gates

The node aura pairs node values `0/1/2` with Sorcery/Nature/Chaos races and grants +2 melee,
ranged, defense and resistance with matching `Gold_*` writes (`0x8FF20..0x8FFF7`). Melee is
ungated; ranged alone requires a positive stat.

Leadership is a per-player maximum read from `[0xC896]`. It reaches only non-Fantastic races and
positive attack fields. Melee takes the byte value directly; non-magical ranged takes the signed
word divided by two with Borland's truncate-toward-zero correction (`0x8FFF7..0x900C5`). Producer
code at `0x9AC0C..0x9ACCC` identifies the two hero-ability tiers and maximum aggregation.

### Holy Bonus and Resistance to All are consumed as side-wide maxima

The recompute reads Holy Bonus from `[0xC89E]` into melee and defense and Resistance to All from
`[0xC89A]` into resistance (`0x900C5..0x9019E`). Every increase has a `Gold_*` twin. CoM 1 alone
extends Holy Bonus to positive ranged attack (`0x900E8..0x900F7`), then fills the saved space with
27 nops. A hero item's Haste power is also copied into the combat-effect word in all builds
(`0x9019E..0x90236`).

### High Prayer supersedes Prayer and the binary uses +2 attack

Both gates require their combat-enchantment byte to equal exactly 1. Prayer gives +1 To Hit,
To Block and Resistance. High Prayer gives those plus +2 melee (only when melee is positive),
+2 Defense and a further +2 Resistance (`0x9024E..0x903A1`). Its terminal jump bypasses Prayer,
so they do not stack. Neither block writes ranged attack. This preserves the existing binary
answer to Q6's unresolved prose conflict: the executable uses +2 melee, not the manual's +3.

### CoM 1 replaces this half's True Light/Darkness block

MoM applies True Light positively to Life and negatively to Death, with Darkness mirrored; the
positive melee/ranged writes require a positive starting attack, while reductions are ungated
(`0x903A1..0x90635`). CoM 1 instead uses the space for Bad Moon, Nature Conjunction, Metal Fires,
Mass Invisibility, Warp Reality, Black Prayer, Guardian and Heavenly Light.

Bad Moon subtracts 2 Resistance. Nature Conjunction adds 2 Defense and Resistance to Fantastic
non-Death units (`0x90306..0x9039A`). Warp Reality is battlefield-wide in this block and subtracts
2 To Hit from non-Chaos units; Black Prayer uses inverted side tests and subtracts 1 melee,
ranged and Defense plus 2 Resistance (`0x904DF..0x90568`). The following `Move_Flags` bit
`0x0400` independently subtracts another To Hit; the bit's meaning remains unknown.

### CoM 1 patched three Metal Fires exclusions out of execution

CoM 1 calls the spell **Blazing March**: its manual lists "Metal Fires → Blazing March" among four
pure renames (`CoM1manual.HTML:21706`), and SPELLDAT.LBX record 117 holds combat-enchantment slot
`0x0A` in every build. `unitcalc.c` uses `CE_BLAZING_MARCH_*`, an alias on the same raw value.

At `0x90405` a former conditional is now unconditional `EB 03`; at `0x90418` the Flame Blade
branch is two nops; and at `0x9045C` the Thrown arm is replaced by unconditional `EB 20`.
Consequently Metal Fires does not exclude Fantastic or Flame Blade units, grants +3 melee when
positive, grants +3 ranged only to missile-class attacks (not Thrown), and ensures
`Weapon_Plus1 >= 1` (`0x903C4..0x90490`).

### The CoM 1 city-defense bytes are Guardian and Heavenly Light

CoM 1 renumbered the retort block, so wizard offset `+0x6A` at `0x90589` is Guardian, not MoM's
old `divine_power` field. The gate requires city combat and the defender, then adds +1 To Hit,
To Block and Resistance (`0x90595..0x905B4`), matching `CoM helptext.txt:2898` clause for clause.

The battlefield byte at raw `+0x1593` is Heavenly Light. ReMoM `Combat.h` places
`city_enchantments` at `+0x157E` and Heavenly Light at slot `0x15`; the shipped helptext
independently matches every guarded write. Defenders gain +1 positive melee/ranged, Defense and
Resistance, weapon To Hit when no weapon quality exists, and `Weapon_Plus1 = 1` when zero
(`0x905BB..0x9064B`). The block reuses R6.1a's `COM1_HOLY_ARMS_TYPE_CEILING` `0x97`.

### CoM 1's relocated tail fell between the original scoped halves

`0x905B8` jumps to a same-frame block at `0x90B8E..0x90C00`; its final instruction at
`0x90BFD` jumps back to `0x905BB`. The latter is therefore live, not residue. R6.1d's scope is
extended to include this disconnected tail so the whole recompute remains assigned.
