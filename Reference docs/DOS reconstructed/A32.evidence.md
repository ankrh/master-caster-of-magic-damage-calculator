# A32 — Shatter eligibility and effect setter

## Scope and result

A32 closes the remaining DOS Shatter eligibility question for calculator versions
`mom_1.31`, `mom_cp_1.60.00`, and `com_6.08`. The frozen executables were:

| build | executable MD5 |
|---|---|
| mom131 | `4123a5e17b7829dcb4457bd3c83cce3d` |
| mom160 | `7700b989590cd71f0535d62c5079e230` |
| com1 | `d92830af2c18fd57e82f26d6c690af82` |

All three builds admit Shatter only for an enemy battle unit whose race is below `0x0F`,
the first Fantastic race. Heroes remain eligible: no Shatter path rejects `Hero_Slot >= 0`,
and all 35 `isHero` records in `Unit rosters/MoM 1.31 unit data.json` have
`baseFantastic: false`. Fantastic units are excluded. This confirms the calculator's existing
normal-unit-plus-hero scope; no calculator behavior, specification, or test change is needed.

The source-shaped admission and setter excerpts are in `combat.c`. The already reconstructed
consumer is in `unitcalc.c` at the Shatter blocks beginning `131/160:0x90ACE` and
`com1:0x907D9`.

## Data binding

The identical executable `Combat_Effects` table begins at `0x2D27E`. Entry 4 at `0x2D2A6`
is `70 44 10 00 00 00 5F 00 E5 01`: name pointer `0x4470` resolves to `Shatter` at
`0x2D910`, and its mask is `0x0010`. In all three `SPELLDAT.LBX` files, record 88 is
Shatter with class byte `+0x15 = 0x10`, Chaos realm byte `+0x17 = 2`, and effect dword
`+0x20 = 0x00000010`. Record 172, Possession, is the only other class-16 record.

## Findings and build differences

1. Human targeting dispatches class 16 to target type 11 and accepts it only for an enemy
   `BATTLE_UNIT.race < 0x0F`. CP assigns Possession type 12, but Shatter remains type 11.
2. AI targeting applies Magic Immunity, the record effect-mask test, controller, active-status,
   visibility, effective-resistance, strength-score, and `race < 0x0F` gates. No Shatter arm
   rejects heroes.
3. MoM 1.31 and CP 1.60 reject Chaos/Death curses, including Shatter, from targets with
   Righteousness. CoM 1 skips this test. CoM instead adds a `Combat_Effects & 0x52` rejection
   for Confusion, Shatter, or Black Sleep; this supplements rather than replaces its record-mask
   test.
4. CoM 1's Shatter-only AI island repeats the race gate and requires melee > 2 or ranged > 2.
   Its AITP prologue also subtracts each valid caster item record's `spell_save` byte from a
   resistance adjustment used in eligibility/scoring. These affect AI target choice, not the
   target kinds a human may choose.
5. CP/CoM's nearby Death-realm hero exclusion and Possession-only Death Immunity check do not
   apply to Chaos-realm Shatter.
6. The generic class-13/16 resolver calls the resistance check and, on success, ORs record
   `+0x20` into `BATTLE_UNIT.Combat_Effects` at `0x822E5`. The recompute consumer is gated only
   by bit `0x0010`, with no race, hero, or unit-type test. CP changes `Grey_Melee` and
   `Grey_Ranged` accounting order but still leaves melee/ranged at 1; eligibility is unchanged.
