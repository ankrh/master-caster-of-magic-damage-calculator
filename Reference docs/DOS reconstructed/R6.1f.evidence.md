## Findings

### CoM Heroism persists a Warlord/Crusade-adjusted level floor

MoM 1.31 and CP 1.60 test Heroism on both the unit and battle-unit enchantment records and raise
only the routine-local level to three (`0x8F89C..0x8F8D7`). They never write
`_UNITS[].Level`.

CoM 1 rewrites that block at `0x8F89C..0x8F8F2`. With Heroism, the floor starts at three; for
unsigned unit types below `0x97`, Warlord at player offset `+0x65` and Crusade at
`Globals[0x11]` each add one. The resulting maximum floor is five, and the routine writes it
back to `_UNITS[].Level` at `0x8F8EE`. Without Heroism, types at or above `0x97` are instead
set persistently to zero; lower types skip the write. The player base `DS:0x9ECA` resolves the
two reads as Warlord at `DS:0x9F2F` and Crusade at `DS:0xA35D`. The `0x97` ceiling is the
normal/fantastic cut shared with Holy Arms and Heavenly Light; R6.1a resolves what it selects.

### The normal and hero ladders are separate, and CoM changes both

MoM's normal ladder is the complete five-step unrolled chain at `0x8FA80..0x8FB3E`:

| Step | Increment |
|---:|---|
| 0 | positive melee, positive ranged, Resistance |
| 1 | Defense, Resistance |
| 2 | positive melee, positive ranged, Resistance, To Hit |
| 3 | Defense, Resistance, To Hit |
| 4 | positive melee, positive ranged, Resistance, To Hit |

CoM replaces it with the 5×7 table at `0x8FACA..0x8FAED`:

| Step | Raw row | Increment |
|---:|---|---|
| 0 | `01 01 00 00 00 00 01` | positive melee, positive ranged, Resistance |
| 1 | `01 01 00 00 00 01 00` | positive melee, positive ranged, Defense |
| 2 | `00 00 00 00 00 01 01` | Defense, Resistance |
| 3 | `01 01 00 00 00 01 00` | positive melee, positive ranged, Defense |
| 4 | `00 00 00 00 01 00 00` | To Hit |

For `ranged_type >= 100`, the ranged-column increment is skipped on every row except step 1
(`0x8FA9A..0x8FAA9`). Thrown, Fire Breath, Lightning Breath, Stoning Gaze, Multiple Gaze and
Death Gaze therefore receive exactly one point. Columns 2 and 3 are zero in every row, so the
computed write at `0x8FABE` never reaches `ranged_type` or ammo. The outer loop tests only
`step < level`; it has no local bound against the five table rows. Exiting it executes the
27-byte NOP slide, jumps over the mana table, then executes the 17-byte NOP slide
(`0x8FAED..0x8FB3E`).

The hero ladder has eight thresholds in every build, but CoM substitutes several writes:

| Step | MoM 1.31 / CP 1.60 | CoM 1 |
|---:|---|---|
| 0 | melee, ranged, Defense, Resistance | melee, ranged, Resistance |
| 1 | melee, ranged, Resistance, To Hit | melee, Defense |
| 2 | melee, ranged, Defense, Resistance | melee, ranged, Resistance |
| 3 | melee, ranged, Resistance | melee, To Hit |
| 4 | melee, ranged, Defense, Resistance, To Hit | melee, ranged, Resistance |
| 5 | melee, ranged, Resistance | melee, Defense |
| 6 | melee, ranged, Defense, Resistance | melee, ranged, Resistance |
| 7 | melee, ranged, Resistance, To Hit | melee, To Hit |

Every melee/ranged write in this table remains gated on that attack strength being positive.
The calculator's shared five-rank control cannot expose this complete hero ladder; F41 owns that
implementation work.

### Hero-template abilities have exact level formulas

The helper re-reads the hero record at every block. Apart from the two Blademaster divisors, MoM
1.31, CP 1.60 and CoM 1 execute the same formulas:

| Ability | Executed effect |
|---|---|
| Noble, or owner's Famous retort | `upkeep = 0` |
| Agility | `Defense += unsigned-byte Level + 1`; the level load is not sign-extended |
| Super Agility | `Defense += ((Level + 1) * 3) / 2`, signed truncation toward zero |
| Blademaster | MoM/CP: `ToHit += (Level + 1) / 2`, signed; CoM: unsigned full-`AX` `/ 3` |
| Super Blademaster | MoM/CP: `ToHit += ((Level + 1) * 3) / 4`; CoM: divisor 6 |
| Might | `melee += unsigned-byte Level + 1`; the level load is not sign-extended |
| Super Might | `melee += ((Level + 1) * 3) / 2`, signed truncation toward zero |
| Arcane Power | when signed `ranged_type / 10 == 3`, `ranged += unsigned-byte Level + 1` |
| Super Arcane Power | same gate, `ranged += ((Level + 1) * 3) / 2` |

CoM Blademaster's `div cl` at `0x8FC7F` divides the full 16-bit `AX`, not AL. For Level at
least −1 it yields the ordinary unsigned quotient; Level at most −2 produces a quotient too large
for AL and raises the divide-error interrupt.

### CoM replaces the hero mana formula with a type table

For positive Casting Skill, MoM 1.31 and CP 1.60 assign

```text
mana_max = (CastingSkill + 1) * (Level + 1) * 5 / 2
```

using signed truncate-toward-zero division (`0x8FE53..0x8FE92`). CoM assigns

```text
mana_max = CastingSkill * (Level + 2) + hero_mana_table[type]
```

at the same destination. The 35-byte table is embedded at `0x8FB0A..0x8FB2D`. The instruction
at `0x8FE74` reads a word at a byte index, but only AL reaches the store, so the effective
addend is the indexed byte; for final index 34 the discarded high byte is the following NOP.
Nonpositive Casting Skill assigns zero in every build.

### CoM turns template Lucky into the ordinary Lucky bit

MoM's template Lucky block directly increments To Hit, To Block, Resistance and Gold Resistance
(`0x8FEC5..0x8FF01`). CoM instead tests and idempotently sets `USA_LUCKY` in
`Attribs_1` (`0x8FEC5..0x8FED6`). This depends on the constructor ordering established by
R6.1b: CoM calls the hero helper before its generic Lucky block, so the newly set bit produces
the same four increments there. MoM runs its generic Lucky block before the helper and therefore
needs the direct increments here.

### Two data tables and their NOP paths share the first routine's extent

CoM embeds both the level table and the hero mana table inside
`BU_Apply_Level_Bonus`'s address extent even though the second table is consumed only by
`BU_Apply_Hero_Abilities`. The level-loop exit path executes both NOP slides around the mana
table. Linear disassembly therefore counts those table bytes as instructions, accounting for
CoM's higher 863-instruction extent count; the reconstruction treats them as data at their actual
read sites.
