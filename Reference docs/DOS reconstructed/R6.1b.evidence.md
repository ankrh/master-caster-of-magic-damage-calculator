# R6.1b — battle-unit constructor

The merged source-shaped reconstruction is in `unitcalc.c`. This item covers the constructor at
`0x8EDFD..0x8F310` in all three builds, excluding the R6.1a relocated tails
(`mom160 0x8F197..0x8F26E`, `com1 0x8F15C..0x8F233`), plus CoM 1's near helper
`0x8F379..0x8F3A7` and its six-byte `cs:0x17D7` table at raw `0x8F3A7..0x8F3AD`.

## Coverage ledgers

Rows are half-open `[Start, End)`. **Numbering restarts at 0 for every disconnected extent**, not
merely for every build: `verify_dos_derivation.py` splits ledgers on that restart alone
(`:79`–`:86`, which also documents that an address discontinuity must *not* split one), so the
restart is what makes it check each of this item's six extents as its own assigned extent.
`Within` therefore cites row numbers local to its own extent.

## Findings

### CoM 1 has three unit-type constructor patches, and the Catapult patch splits mutation state

Before the shared type-table initialization, CoM 1 special-cases three persistent unit records:

- type `0xAE` (Zombies) starts from `toblock = 0` and decrements it to −1
  (`0x8EE28`–`0x8EE31`);
- type `0x51` (Golem) adds Resist Elements to `item_enchantments`
  (`0x8EE39`–`0x8EE40`);
- type `0x25` (Catapult) with `wp == 9` writes `_UNITS[si].mutations = 1`
  (`0x8EEA4`–`0x8EEAF`).

The Catapult write occurs after the constructor snapshots mutations into `[bp-7]` at
`0x8EE21`, but weapon quality performs a fresh record read at `0x8F024`. The unit therefore
takes quality 1 immediately, while the earlier `BU_Apply_Specials` argument at `0x8F0D6`
still receives the stale snapshot. This is an intentional two-value flow in the bytes, not one
local reused twice.

### Later builds import type attributes, but CP 1.60 preserves the high Abilities byte

MoM 1.31 initializes the five base stats from the unit-type table without re-copying its movement
or ability flags. CP 1.60 and CoM 1 additionally import Move_Flags' low byte, Attribs_1's low byte
plus bit 8, and Attribs_2 bits `0x28` (`160:0x8EEB0`–`0x8EEFD`;
`com1:0x8EEBE`–`0x8EF0B`). CP loads and stores only the low byte of Abilities
(`0x8EEB5` `8A 87 BA 01`; `0x8EEF4` `26 88 47 1C`), preserving the existing high byte.
CoM loads and stores the full word (`0x8EEC3` `8B 87 BA 01`; `0x8EF02`
`26 89 47 1C`).

### CoM 1 adds a three-entry mana override table

Both MoM builds write `mana_max = 40` for Attribs_1 `0x4000` and then `20` for
`0x2000`, leaving the field untouched if neither bit is present (`0x8EF37`–`0x8EF5D`).
CoM first makes that a single unconditional 0/40/20 store (`0x8EF45`–`0x8EF5B`), then calls
the near helper at `0x8EF5F`.

The helper's `cs:0x17D7` resolves to raw `0x8F3A7`; its bytes are
`B1 18 3E 0E C3 23`, three `(type, mana)` pairs:

| CoM type | Name | Final `mana_max` |
|---:|---|---:|
| `0xB1` | Angel | 24 |
| `0x3E` | Apprentices | 14 |
| `0xC3` | Djinn | 35 |

The exact CoM Attribs_1 record words are respectively `0x6208`, `0x6000`, and `0x6200`;
all carry the two caster bits. The separate helper override, not an absence of caster flags,
explains their nonstandard final mana.

### Constructor ordering differs in CoM 1

MoM runs Lucky (`0x8EF6C`) before the hero/item blocks (`0x8EFB7`), while CoM runs the
hero/item blocks first (`0x8EF70`) and Lucky second (`0x8EFAE`). The order is observable
because both paths can write Weapon_Plus1 and combat modifiers.

More importantly, MoM runs Chaos Surge before `BU_Apply_Specials` and calls that routine at
`0x8F2A2`; CoM calls it at `0x8F0E8` and runs Chaos Surge afterwards at `0x8F0EE`.
That confirms the order-sensitive Chaos Channels result already documented in
`MoM binary analysis.md`: the MoM fire-breath assignment overwrites the Surge ranged bonus,
where CoM's Surge lands after the assignment.

### CP 1.60 and CoM 1 remove 1.31's Flight movement floor differently

After the movement call, 1.31 recomputes enchantments and raises `movement_points` to 6 when it
is below 3 under Flight (`0x8F2B6`–`0x8F2F8`). CP still carries the old bytes, but
`0x8F2B6` is `EB 45`, so execution jumps directly to the hit-point call and the whole floor is
dead. CoM nops the floor out and, unlike both MoM builds, performs no `movement_points` write in
this constructor at all: it replaces the far `0x03C8:0x0043` movement call/store with a bare
near call to `0x90B11` at `0x8F2A3`.

### Survival Instinct is the CoM player byte at DS:0xA363

CoM tests `players[bu->controller_idx].survival_instinct` (`DS:0xA363`) and `race > 0x0E` at
`0x8F277`–`0x8F284`, then grants +2 Resistance, +1 To Hit and +1 Defense, with matching
Gold_Resist and Gold_Defense writes (`0x8F286`–`0x8F29E`). The eligibility gate and exact
magnitudes identify the byte as **Survival Instinct**: both `CoM helptext.txt:194` and
`CoM1manual.HTML:6803`–`:6814` say that the caster's fantastic creatures receive those three
bonuses. The controller index, rather than the persistent owner index, selects whose global
enchantment applies during combat.

### Grey_Hits is deliberately outside the constructor's zeroing run

All three builds clear `melee_tohit`, `ranged_tohit`, `toblock`, and the
`Gold_*`/`Grey_*` bytes `+0x64..+0x6C`, but they skip `Grey_Hits` at `+0x6D`.
All builds initialize Gold_Hits to zero; only CP 1.60 and CoM 1 later store the hit-point
routine's `DL` result there (`160:0x8F2F7`, `com1:0x8F306`). MoM 1.31 stores only `AL`
to `hits` after that call (`0x8F306`).
