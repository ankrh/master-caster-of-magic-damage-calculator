# D37 — CoM 6.08 post-combat result materialization

## Scope, identity, and strengthened criterion

Method 4 package `D37`: two independent Codex GPT-5.6 Sol High derivations were merged, mechanically checked, and reviewed once by Claude Opus 5 Medium. All 19 review findings were byte-verified and incorporated; none remained disputed. Calculator behavior is unchanged because calculator scope is not applicable.

This evidence covers only `WIZARDS.EXE` raw `[0x9BCE0,0x9D535)`.  It does not use bytes at or
after `0x9D535` to reconstruct the excluded next routine.  Calculator versions are not applicable.

Identity was checked directly:

- file size: `1,006,232` bytes;
- MD5: `d92830af2c18fd57e82f26d6c690af82`;
- SHA-256: `2c5f3be497a2790eb3a76722ec5ccd5ba8505242b042b9994ac054c4aa798988`;
- extent length: `6,229` bytes;
- extent SHA-256: `f7f19608db94c4f058916e831610147792ebde0b5cff7d4086ea151413cfc75f`.

I strengthened the starting criteria with one non-scope-changing requirement: **classify retained
but unreachable instruction islands separately from live semantics**.  Rationale: raw unconditional
jumps at `0x9BF6E`/`0x9C917` and long NOP patches otherwise make an older source-shaped reading look
live when it is not.  No starting criterion was weakened.

The main entry has `push bp; mov bp,sp; sub sp,0x74; push si; push di` at `0x9BCE0..0x9BCE8`.
The sole far exit is `pop di; pop si; mov sp,bp; pop bp; retf` at `0x9D52F..0x9D535`.
The internal near returns are `0x9BE1F`, `0x9C082`, `0x9C0FB`, and `0x9C121`.  Linear decoding
ends exactly at `0x9D535`; bytes there begin a distinct `55 8B EC` prologue and are excluded.

## ABI and incoming/outgoing closure

Best raw-backed name: `End_Of_Combat` (the routine finalizes combat-unit material state, city/lair
aftermath, items, fame/experience, results UI, and survivor damage).  This is a post-combat *routine
name*, not an assumption that the `0x9C26D` test has a particular phase or that its subject has a
post-combat identity.

Both overlay `$03D8:$0020` call sites push four near words right-to-left and remove eight bytes:

| caller | pushes before call | cleanup | raw consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `0x6C3C4` | `[bp-0x34]`, `[bp+0x16]`, `[bp+0x14]`, `[bp-0x2E]` at `0x6C3B8..0x6C3C1` | `add sp,8` at `0x6C3C9` | last pushed value was returned by `$0348:$0020` or loaded from `DS:[0xC434]`; it is compared with `DS:[0xC586]` after return |
| `0x7FC98` | `[bp-8]`, `[bp+0x12]`, `[bp+0x10]`, `[bp-2]` at `0x7FC8C..0x7FC95` | `add sp,8` at `0x7FC9D` | last pushed value is selected as `DS:[0xC586]` or `DS:[0xC584]` and compared with `DS:[0xC586]` after return |

The callee binds them without ambiguity:

```c
void far End_Of_Combat(
    int16_t winner_player_idx,       /* [bp+06] */
    int16_t *recovered_item_count,   /* [bp+08] */
    int16_t recovered_items[],       /* [bp+0A] */
    int16_t result_message_type);    /* [bp+0C] */
```

`[bp+06]` is compared to battle-unit `controller_idx`, the two side globals `DS:[0xC586]` and
`DS:[0xC584]`, human `0`, neutral `5`, and `DS:[0xBD9C]` player count; it is written to both
`s_UNIT.owner_idx` and `BATTLE_UNIT.controller_idx`.  Thus argument 0 is the winning player index,
including sentinel/neutral values.  `[bp+08]` is dereferenced as a count, cleared, bounded by `18`,
and incremented; `[bp+0A]+2*count` receives item indices; `[bp+0C]` is copied to `DS:[0xC97E]`
and tested against `4` and `6`-class result values.

An executable-wide byte scan for `9A 20 00 D8 03`, followed by aligned decoding, finds exactly the
two calls above.  The overlay descriptor resolves segment index `0x3D8/8=123`, slot 0 to raw
`0x9BCE0`.  Byte-level relative-edge candidates from outside the extent all decode as data or
misaligned targets; there is no other aligned external near entry.  Internal calls are
`0x9D3B6→0x9BE04`, `0x9BDBC→0x9C077`, `0x9C600→0x9C0C1`, and
`0x9C8EC→0x9C114`.  The live main flow jumps over every helper body.  Outgoing calls are fully
inventoried below; the only call whose target is exactly the excluded next boundary is
`0x9D50E→0x9D535`.

### Bounded producer/consumer dependencies

`0x9E29A` is a BP-sharing external helper called at `0x9C1DC`.  Bounded raw decoding shows it
finds the maximum `(Level+1)` among winner-controlled active units with item-enchantment byte
`[+0x2F]&0x10`, multiplies it by `0x008C`, then consumes that pool against winner-controlled dead
units (`status=4`, nonhero, not `UM_UNDEAD`) in battle order.  Admitted units become `status=5`,
their controller changes to the opposite combat side, and `Attribs_1|=0x10`.  This is the immediate
producer of a subset of the `BUS_DRAINED` candidates consumed at `0x9C1F3`; its broader design name
is not needed and remains bounded.

The `0x9C26D` Supernatural/Nominal-Skill gate occurs only after controller, ability, status,
combat-effect, `Attribs_1`, and nonhero filters.  It neither proves who caused the drained state nor
assigns a distinct post-combat identity.  The complete material transition is the write sequence
`0x9C2B7..0x9C377`: restore figures, clear front damage, mark `Attribs_1`, set Death race, place the
underlying unit at combat coordinates, assign winner ownership/controller, set `UM_UNDEAD`, count
it, add two experience points, and activate it. The overflow state `0x0C` then re-enters at
`0x9C1DF`, after the one-time external producer, and repeats retreat/material scanning when
survivor count reaches ten.

## Exit, order, and terminal behavior

There is one far entry and one far return.  No semantic branch can leave the routine early: every
live main-flow exit converges on `0x9D52F`, which restores `DI`, `SI`, and `SP/BP`, then executes the
sole `retf` at `0x9D534`.  Both callers perform caller cleanup with `add sp,8`, proving the four-word
Pascal/C-style far-call frame described above. The four internal BP-sharing helpers each return
near to their one live caller and do not adjust the inherited outer frame or arguments; as
BP-sharing machine-code helpers they may clobber caller registers, including the automatic-raze
helper's `SI`, `ES:BX`, `CX`, and `AX` result/scratch state.

Material processing is ordered and deterministic.  The first battle-unit pass runs increasing
indices `0..COMBAT_TOTAL-1`; the bounded producer at `0x9E29A` likewise consumes eligible dead units
in battle order. In the first pass, the retained `status==FLEE` and `status==RECALLED` comparisons
at `0x9BFCF`/`0x9BFE3` cannot succeed on the live fallthrough because `status<=DEAD` already jumped
at `0x9BFC0`; they are redundant compiler residue, although the instructions execute for larger
statuses. The orphan jump at `0x9C405..0x9C408` is unreachable after the unconditional short jump
at `0x9C403`. The drained-unit conversion loop again scans increasing indices, applies every
