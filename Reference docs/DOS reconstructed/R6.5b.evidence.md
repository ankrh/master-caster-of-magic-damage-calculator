# R6.5b evidence — battle-unit healing and temporary Hits

This is the durable evidence companion to [`combat.c`](combat.c) for exported VROOMM target
`0370:002A`, reconstructed there as `Battle_Unit_Heal`. Codex Agent A and Codex Agent B
independently reconstructed the routine across all three pinned DOS builds, then reciprocally
reviewed both artifacts. Every review entry was resolved from the quoted bytes and no disagreement
survived.

## Findings

`Battle_Unit_Heal` has two modes selected by its third word argument. With `temp_hits == 0`, it
caps `healing` at the zero-extended sum of Regular and Undeath damage (`0x7FCF7..0x7FD08`). A
nonzero mode does not apply that cap. The Life Steal caller at `0x9A18E..0x9A19B` passes the
resistance-failure margin as `healing` and raw mode `1`.

The common damage-channel path subtracts the full signed healing word from the Regular local,
spills a negative remainder into Undeath, clamps both at zero, and assigns all three low bytes
back to the record. It does not reduce Irreversible damage on this path
(`0x7FD0B..0x7FD99`). Separately, it subtracts only the low byte of healing from signed
`front_figure_damage`. A negative result restores figures one at a time and adds signed per-figure
Hits to the 16-bit remainder (`0x7FD99..0x7FE52`).

MoM 1.31 and CoM 1 permit restoration up to raw `Max_Figures`. CP 1.60 first computes unsigned
`irreversible_figures = damage[2] / hits` and permits restoration only up to the signed-byte
comparison against `Max_Figures - irreversible_figures` (`0x7FCE0..0x7FCEF`,
`0x7FE2C..0x7FE50`). When restoration leaves a positive top remainder, all builds store its low
byte as front-figure damage. CP and CoM additionally clear the local's low byte before continuing;
1.31 retains the positive local (`0x7FE52..0x7FE68`).

After an ordinary-mode negative remainder is clamped to zero, the routine takes the absolute value
of the local. A nonzero mode therefore turns remaining negative over-heal into a positive value.
If that value reaches the build's threshold, the quotient becomes per-figure `Extra_Hits`. In
1.31, the threshold and signed divisor are raw `Max_Figures`, and the quotient's low byte is added
with byte wrap (`0x7FE6C..0x7FEE2`). Because 1.31 also retains a positive post-restoration local,
that value reaches the same Extra-Hits test even without a negative over-heal remainder.

CP uses byte `effective_max = Max_Figures - irreversible_figures`, compares it and the absolute
value as signed low bytes, rejects zero, and then performs unsigned `AX / effective_max`. It adds
the quotient byte to `Extra_Hits`, then calculates
`quotient * irreversible_figures + old damage[2]`; a signed word above 200 has AL replaced by 200,
and AL is stored to Irreversible damage (`0x7FE89..0x7FEC6`). CoM uses the signed raw-Max threshold
but zero-extends Max for its signed word division, sign-extends old `Extra_Hits`, and caps the new
signed sum at 90 (`0x7FE89..0x7FECE`).

Every path then saves signed-byte `movement_points`, calls the reconstructed constructor and stat
recompute through `03A0:003E` and `03A0:0052`, and restores the saved movement byte
(`0x7FEE2..0x7FF3D`).
