# B4/B5/B6 — DOS damage rollover and Defense package

## Scope and identity

This package verifies three linked calculator assumptions: figure-boundary rollover and fresh
Defense (B4), Armor Piercing rounding plus Immolation's exclusion (B5), and repeated
Invulnerability subtraction (B6).

| Build | WIZARDS.EXE MD5 | Calculator version |
|---|---|---|
| MoM 1.31 | `4123a5e17b7829dcb4457bd3c83cce3d` | `mom_1.31` |
| MoM CP 1.60 | `7700b989590cd71f0535d62c5079e230` | `mom_cp_1.60.00` |
| CoM 1 / CoM 6.08 | `d92830af2c18fd57e82f26d6c690af82` | `com_6.08` |

Modern calculator versions are not in scope. The assigned raw executable extents were:

- B4: conventional rollover `[0x9A387,0x9A53D)` and spell resolution
  `[0x871C6,0x87356)`;
- B5: Immolation dispatch `[0x99D3D,0x99D73)`, spell flag/Defense setup
  `[0x8711A,0x871C6)`, and Armor Piercing `[0x9A769,0x9A779)`;
- B6: the B4 subranges `[0x9A499,0x9A4CE)` and `[0x872BF,0x87313)`.

The supplemental data extent is Fireball's complete `SPELLDAT.LBX` record
`[0x0FA4,0x0FC8)` in each build. The source-shaped bodies already merged by R6.2c/e/f in
[combat.c](combat.c) were checked against the new package derivation and required no correction.

## Derivation and review provenance

This was a one-time salvage of the already-running pre-Method-4 package, authorized by the user
after B4, B5 and B6 had mistakenly been dispatched as separate paid sessions. One cold Codex
derivation and one persisted Claude Opus derivation covered each component. The main Codex agent
merged all six reads into one package, then one newly launched Claude Opus 5 High session reviewed
that merged Codex derivation against the raw binaries and spell records.

The review confirmed every behavioral conclusion. It identified two actionable rendering defects:
the B4 derivation had conflated the local `[bp-0x28]` special-damage array with the caller's result
array, and B4/B6 had rendered the spell rollover as `do/while` despite the entry jump to its test.
Both were corrected to match `combat.c`'s existing `local_damage` and pre-tested `while`. Three
minor naming/type findings were also accepted. All revised component/build checker runs passed;
no disagreement survived.

## Findings

### B4 — rollover and Area damage

Conventional damage re-enters the whole-figure path through the back edge
`0x9A4EA -> 0x9A3B4`. After a full figure is consumed, `0x9A4A7` calls `CMB_DefenseRoll` again
before the next figure receives the remainder. The same rule applies to non-Area spell damage:
`0x872BD` jumps first to the comparison at `0x872FF`, and `0x87313 -> 0x872BF` enters the body only
while the remainder exceeds a figure's hits. Each crossed boundary calls Defense again at
`0x872E3`.

Area spells follow a different route. Flag `0x1000` selects one outer attack per current figure and
sets internal damage-limit flag `0x2000` (`0x871C6..0x871E7`). The arm
`0x87299..0x872BB` caps each outer result to one full figure and jumps directly to accumulation;
it never enters the rollover loop. Area overkill is therefore discarded within that per-figure
attack rather than carried to another figure.

### B5 — Armor Piercing and Immolation

At `0x9A769..0x9A779`, all three executables use:

```text
mov ax,si; cwd; sub ax,dx; sar ax,1; mov si,ax
```

This is signed division by two with truncation toward zero. Nonnegative odd Defense rounds down;
negative odd values round up toward zero. Calculator Defense is nonnegative at this point, so
`Math.trunc(n / 2)` and the former floor operation have identical reachable results; the
implementation now states the exact DOS arithmetic.

Immolation dispatches spell ID 96, Fireball, with strength 4 in MoM 1.31/CP 1.60 and strength 10
in CoM 1. Fireball's record flags are `0x1000`: Area is set and Armor Piercing bit `0x0001` is
clear. `Apply_Battle_Unit_Damage_From_Spell` loads that word directly at `0x8713D`; the dispatch
passes no initiating attack flags. Immolation therefore cannot inherit the attacker's Armor
Piercing ability. CoM 1's apparent spell-ID-7 payload at `0x99D5F` has no incoming instruction
edge and is unreachable patch residue. Its Defense-special context literal `0x27`, versus
`0x26` in the MoM builds, follows the same side of every relevant callee comparison.

### B6 — repeated Invulnerability

The Invulnerability enchantment mask is the dword `0x80000000`, represented by the split-word
tests at `0x9A4AF`, `0x87251`, and `0x872EC`. After every fresh Defense roll, the engine subtracts
2 damage (`0x9A4BF` for conventional rollover; `0x872FC` for spell rollover). The figure-boundary
back edges re-execute both operations for every newly exposed figure. Automatic/Doom damage is
the sole exception: `0x9A49E` and `0x872DB` bypass Defense and Invulnerability together.

## Version differences

- `[0x871C6,0x87356)`, `[0x9A769,0x9A779)`, and both B6 subranges are byte-identical across all
  three builds.
- In `[0x9A387,0x9A53D)`, only three result-bucket mask immediates differ: `0x0020` in MoM 1.31
  and `0x0060` in CP 1.60/CoM 1. They do not change rollover or Defense behavior.
- In `[0x8711A,0x871C6)`, CoM 1 changes only the spell Defense context literal from `0x26` to
  `0x27`; it is inert for the package conclusions.
- Immolation dispatch differs in gating and strength, but every live arm still calls Fireball and
  therefore remains Area and non-Armor-Piercing.

## Calculator result

The verified engine behavior matches `singleAttackDmgDist`, `areaPerFigureDmgDist`, the DOS
Defense profile, and the existing Immolation/Invulnerability regression presets for all three
scoped calculator versions. The only implementation edit is the behavior-preserving replacement
of floor with truncation in DOS Armor Piercing so the source expresses the exact signed operation.
