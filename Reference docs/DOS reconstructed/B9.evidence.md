## Result and why the extracted rosters are insufficient

The extracted unit rosters describe `unit_types[]`: static `0x24`-byte type-definition records.
They establish a type's race, base abilities, stats and intrinsic immunities. Dispel Evil does not
use that table to decide whether its extra undead penalty applies. It reads byte `+0x17` of the
dynamic `0x20`-byte `_UNITS[]` instance record: `mutations & UM_UNDEAD (0x20)`.

`Create_Unit` initializes that instance byte to zero for every type, including natural Death-realm
creatures. Its city path can subsequently replace the byte only with weapon quality `0..3`, set
Magic Weapons `0x01`, or OR one Chaos Channels bit (`0x04`, `0x08`, or `0x10`). Its non-city path,
used by summons and other creations without a city, bypasses every later mutation write and changes
only XP and Level. No constructor path tests the unit type's race or realm, and none can introduce
`UM_UNDEAD`.

Therefore natural Death creatures do **not** receive the created-undead-only extra Dispel Evil
penalty. That bit is instance provenance/state written by explicit conversion mechanics such as
Create Undead or Blood Lust, not an intrinsic fact recoverable from the roster. The calculator's
existing split between natural Death creatures and created undead is correct for all three scoped
calculator versions; no calculator implementation change is required.

`0x97D32 -> 0x97FC7` for values at least 2000. The tail `0x97FC7..0x9800C` writes only XP and

## Other recovered build differences

- Capacity gates are 950 / 980 / 1000 in MoM 1.31 and 1950 / 1980 / 2000 in CP 1.60 and CoM 1.
  The hard gate is equality, not greater-than-or-equal.
- `TBL_Experience` at raw `0x2B214` is `0,20,60,120,200,300,450,600,1000` in both MoM builds
  and `0,30,80,180,300,550,900,1400,2000` in CoM 1.
- CoM's Evil Presence suppresses its Barracks and War College XP grants but not Altar of Battle.
  Its Alchemy condition adds a city-race-nonzero conjunct at `0x97F56..0x97F5E`.
- The global byte at `DS:0xA357` is `Globals[0x0B]`, Doom Mastery. Chaos Surge is the adjacent
  `DS:0xA356` byte.
- The fortress scan runs when signed city owner `< num_players`; neutral/raider cities go directly
  to `Destroy_City`. A fortress refusal restores population but leaves `Pop_10s = 3`.
- CoM copies city race to unit byte `+0x1E`, substitutes race-specific Settlers type IDs, and can
  set `UE_ORIHALCON` from the high byte of `City_Best_Weapon`.

The semantics of CoM's relocated islands, `DS:0xBD90`, and unit byte `+0x13` remain outside B9's
claimed result. Their byte coverage and register/flag contracts are preserved in `unitcalc.c`.
