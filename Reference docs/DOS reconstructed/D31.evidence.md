# D31 — CoM 6.08 Raise Dead identity locate

Package and method: [`D30_D31_D32`](./D30_D31_D32.evidence.md), Method 4. Calculator scope:
`com_6.08`. This is evidence-only; calculator behavior is unchanged.

## Scope correction and spell identity

The frozen row named `MAGIC.EXE`, but the repository's authoritative CoM 6.08 implementation
binary is `WIZARDS.EXE`, 1,006,232 bytes, MD5 `d92830af2c18fd57e82f26d6c690af82`; see
[`MoM binary analysis.md`](../MoM%20binary%20analysis.md). The complete frozen `MAGIC.EXE`
frontier contains neither the direct `$89` dispatch nor the identity-write run. The durable locate
below is corrective evidence in the right module, not a silent substitution inside the frozen
scope.

CoM 6.08 `SPELLDAT.LBX`, 8,288 bytes, MD5 `b57bfafec2566306b25ac74bce1bd770`, uses D22's
zero-based `0x224 + spell_id * 0x24` record layout. Record 137 begins at `0x1568` with the
NUL-padded name `Raise Dead`; record 136 at `0x1544` is `Unicorns`. Thus dispatcher value
`$89 = 137` is proved from CoM-era data rather than transferred from the modern spell map.

## Routine boundary, dispatch, and exits

The complete far routine is `[0xAB04D,0xAB474)`, 1,063 bytes, SHA-256
`ac78270902a0b180d3739540a609d746baa609cfb0a6b48c7b141ce9100fbe04`:

- prologue `55 8B EC 83 EC 24 56 57` at `0xAB04D`;
- the failed-selection edge `0xAB1BE: E9 9E 02` reaches common cleanup at `0xAB45F`;
- common epilogue `5F 5E 8B E5 5D CB` has the routine's only `retf`, at `0xAB473`;
- the next prologue begins exactly at `0xAB474`.

The dispatcher slice `[0x82ED0,0x82EEA)`, SHA-256
`b6e1b3ca535eee2a58727c302b7e31f045da6ce38ada9729e370bb3f33cdfe04`, contains
`81 FF 89 00` (`cmp di,$89`), `75 14`, and the direct far call
`0x82EE2: 9A 48 00 18 04`. Overlay resolution maps segment 131, slot 8, thunk `$48` to
`0xAB04D`. That call is the only whole-image direct far-call encoding of the target; constructed
indirect far pointers remain outside the direct-call proof.

## Success-path identity writes

The success path halves figures/front damage and clears combat effects and the enchantment dword,
matching Raise Dead's documented revive shape. It then writes:

```text
AB2B8  26 C6 47 0B 15   race := rt_Fantastic_No_Realm ($15)
AB2BD  26 80 67 19 7F   Attribs_1 &= ~USA_UNKNOWN_8000
AB2C2  26 C6 47 6D FF   Grey_Hits := -1
```

`Grey_Hits = -1` is a movement-cache sentinel consumed by the already reconstructed CoM 1 unit
calculation, not a No-Heal marker. More importantly, DOS Raise Dead writes the unaligned
Fantastic identity inline. The modern premise—write a No-Heal combat enchantment and let
recalculation derive identity—does not describe this path. The bounded mutation list does not yet
settle whether another instruction in the full routine also sets an enchantment flag.
