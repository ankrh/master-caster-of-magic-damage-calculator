## Findings

### CP 1.60 makes three surgical corrections but leaves one candidate block disabled

The shared MoM path applies Metal Fires, Mass Invisibility, Warp Reality and Black Prayer
identically (`0x90635..0x9085D`). CP 1.60 then replaces 1.31's unknown `Combat_Effects 0x0400`
block with Black Sleep and a player-byte `+0x6A` probe. Unconditional jumps at `0x90885` and
`0x90891` discard the probe and skip all four candidate bonus writes; the offset's CP retort name
is not established. CP also restores Weakness's raw Thrown comparison by nopping the second `/10`
at `0x90917`, and it fixes both Shatter accumulator arms by crediting the pre-clamp loss before
storing 1 (`0x90AE5..0x90B1C`).

### MoM 1.31's unknown `0x0400` reduction credits the bonus accumulator

The block at `0x90860` subtracts one To Hit and one Resistance, but its final read/write uses
field displacement `+0x67`, `Gold_Resist`, not `Grey_Resist` (`0x9088B..0x90894`). The effect bit
has no established semantic name. This is the one reduction in the reconstructed routine that
credits a gold accumulator and is evidence for the display-tier classification.

### Vertigo, Weakness and Mind Storm differ precisely by build

MoM Vertigo subtracts 2 To Hit and 1 Defense; CoM 1 subtracts 3 To Hit and 1 To Block
(`0x9089B..0x908D3`, CoM `0x90651..0x90662`). Weakness is −2 melee and missile in 1.31; its
second `/10` makes the apparent Thrown arm unreachable. CP restores Thrown. CoM 1 uses −3 melee
and applies −3 ranged to every decade class through magic plus raw Thrown
(`0x90662..0x906C2`). Mind Storm is −5 to all four stats in MoM; CoM 1 softens melee alone to
−3 and retains −5 ranged, Defense and Resistance (`0x90942..0x909CB`, CoM
`0x906C2..0x906F4`).

### Warp Creature and Shatter expose both arithmetic and ordering differences

MoM Warped Attack and Defense leave the ordinary nonnegative stat at half; CoM 1 halves melee
and the shared ranged slot with an arithmetic byte shift and divides Defense by 3 with signed
truncate-toward-zero division (`0x90A23..0x90AAD`, CoM `0x90749..0x90795`). Warped Resist sets
Resistance to zero in every build. CoM 1 therefore also halves Thrown, Breath and gaze values that
share its ranged slot.

Shatter immediately follows. MoM 1.31 and CoM 1 store the stat to 1 before re-reading it for the
`Grey_*` assignment, so those twin fields receive zero (`0x90AE5..0x90B18`, CoM
`0x907F0..0x9081A`). CP 1.60 alone accumulates the actual loss. The recompute itself tests only
`BUE_SHATTER`; any CoM 1 normal/hero eligibility restriction must occur where the effect bit is set,
not here.

### CoM 1 Supreme Light has five eligibility paths and an unidentified movement bit

An active unit on the enchanted side qualifies through any of: live magical ranged type
`30 < ranged_type < 100`, Life race, nonzero mana, persistent Focus Magic, or unit-type base
`30 <= ranged_type < 100` (`0x90992..0x90A29`). The block adds 2 melee unconditionally, adds 2
ranged only when the shared ranged stat is positive, adds signed `Resistance / 3` to Defense, and
sets raw `Move_Flags 0x0100` (`0x90A29..0x90A5C`). The shipped helptext promises post-combat
regeneration, making that bit a plausible carrier, but no read site was traced and the binary does
not establish its meaning here.

### CoM 1 Realm Wards use −2/−3/−3, not the helptext's −2/−4/−4

City-enchantment indices 9 through 13 map Nature, Sorcery, Chaos, Life and Death by
`race - rt_Nature`. A matching Fantastic unit loses 2 To Hit, 3 Defense and 3 Resistance
(`0x90A5C..0x90AA0`). All five shipped helptext entries instead say 2 To Hit, 4 Defense and
4 Resistance. The binary and shipped text therefore conflict.

### CoM 1 Tactician and Eternal Night are late post-Warp writes

Tactician grants every controlled unit +1 Defense. Heroes receive a second Defense point plus
+2 melee, shared ranged and Resistance (`0x90AA0..0x90B00`). The hero test reads frame-local
`si`, captured from `bu->unit_idx` at the routine head. The near Eternal Night helper then loops
over every player and subtracts one Resistance from a non-Death unit for each other owner with
the global active (`0x90B11..0x90B41`). Its bottom-tested loop processes player 0 before reading
`num_players`.

### CoM 1's relocated tail consumes three side-wide hero maxima

Before Heavenly Light, `0x90B8E..0x90BFD` reads per-controller maxima produced at
`0x9AB14..0x9ABB0`. Guiding Beacon adds its value to a positive conventional ranged attack
(`0 < ranged_type < 100`); Divine Barrier adds its value to Defense without a unit gate; Soul
Linker applies only to Fantastic units and splits its value as `ceil(v/2)` To Hit and `floor(v/2)`
To Block. The tail then recovers the battle-unit index and calls unidentified far routine
`0x03E0:0x003E` before returning to `0x905BB`.
