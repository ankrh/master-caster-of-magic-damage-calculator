## Branch inventory

The item helper's repeated three-way long tests are compiler output, but every edge is accounted
for. In MoM low-word blocks 1–9 and 12, the high word is forced to zero: the `jl` and `jg` edges
are dead and the `jbe` edge is live. In high-word blocks 10–11, 13–18 and 20, `jl` is dead while
`jg` and `jbe` are live. Invulnerability uses an unsigned form: its `jb` is dead and `ja`/`jbe`
are live. CoM retains the same meanings except where explicitly compacted or replaced.

## Findings

### Item powers and Q22

- The helper tests exactly twenty input masks. In both MoM builds nineteen copy their bit to
  `item_enchantments`; Holy Avenger input `0x00200000` instead grants Bless `0x02000000`.
- CoM 1 changes Path Finding `0x00000080` into Land Link: it ORs `Move_Flags` low byte `0x60`
  and still contributes item-enchantment bit `0x00000080`.
- CoM 1 repurposes the old Magic Immunity input `0x00080000` to `Move_Flags 0x0200`; it does not
  contribute an item-enchantment bit.
- CoM 1 repurposes Righteousness input `0x40000000` as the manual's Shadow item power. It grants
  Thrown (`ranged_type = 100`) when the live unit is already Thrown or its base type has no
  ranged attack, then sets ranged strength to signed melee shifted right once. The bare `sar`
  floors negative odd values; it is not C signed division.
- The helper never tests CoM Inner Fire's raw `0x08000000` bit. Together with the NOP-filled old
  Giant Strength melee and ranged write blocks in R6.1e, this proves that the hero-item pipeline
  contains no implementation of the manual-promised +1 attack. Q22 is therefore closed as a
  negative binary finding, not as a calculator behavior to implement.

### Recompute hit points

- The routine unions persistent `_UNITS.enchantments`, live `bu->enchantments`, and
  `bu->item_enchantments`, then applies Heroism's level-three floor in every build.
- Only 1.31 retains Crusade's extra level for a non-Fantastic unit type. Its controller byte is
  sign-extended by bare opcode `0x98` (`cbw`) before indexing players. CP and CoM replace the
  entire 59-byte block with NOPs.
- Base Hits is a signed byte. 1.31 Black Channels adds one hit and one Gold Hit; CP keeps only
  the hit; CoM removes the block. Lion Heart is +3 in both MoM builds, but CP gates its Gold-Hits
  part on the live battle-unit source. CoM changes the hit bonus to unsigned `8 / Max_Figures`
  and keeps CP's live-source Gold-Hits gate.
- Hero detection still uses persistent `Hero_Slot > -1`. Constitution and Super Constitution
  come from the owner-specific hero template; Super Constitution uses signed truncate-toward-zero
  `((level + 1) * 3) / 2`. Charm of Life uses the persistent owner and grants `max(hits / 4, 1)`.
- `Extra_Hits` is sign-extended when added to returned hits but its raw byte is added to
  `Gold_Hits`.

### CoM `Battle_Unit_Moves2`

- The far routine is the target `0x03E0:0x003E` called at `0x90BE9`. It and its two private near
  helpers are complete; all twelve calls remain inside the assigned helper pair.
- Web HP above zero returns zero. Otherwise movement begins with signed type `Move_Halves`,
  while the unit-type index and controller index are unsigned bytes. Mystic Surge adds six
  half-points; three hero items add their signed `moves2` bytes and can supply Endurance.
- The item slot word gate is signed `item > -1`, but the subsequent item-record index is
  truncated to a signed byte by `B8 3200; F6 2F` before the 50-byte multiplication.
- The byte table at `DS:0x3AC8` is the per-controller Logistics maximum written by the R6.1d
  battlefield scan. This consumer does not settle how wizard levels produce the table value:
  CoM helptext says +0.5 movement per two levels while the manual says +0.5 per level (Q23).
  Flight floors movement at six half-points, Chaos Channels wings at four, Endurance adds two,
  and Haste doubles the result.
- Combat-enchantment slots `0x18`/`0x19`, paired with player words at `DS:0xC584`/`0xC586`, are
  Entangle: they subtract four half-points from a matched corporeal unit unless its live runtime
  enchantments include Wraith Form. This matches CoM's prose effect of losing two movement.
- The result is clamped to zero. CoM repurposes `Grey_Hits +0x6D` as the cached prior maximum.
  Sentinel `-1` initializes current movement and the cache. Equality returns without rewriting
  either field. On change, an 8-bit delta adjusts current movement unless Move_Flags `0x1000`
  suppresses it; `0x0800` halves the signed delta with a bare arithmetic shift. The cache is
  stored on sentinel and changed-value paths only.
