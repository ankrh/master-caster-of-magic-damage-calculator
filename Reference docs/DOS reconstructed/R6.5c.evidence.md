This is the merged evidence for the overland movement routine exported through operand
`03C8:0043`. All three inputs were checked against the authoritative binary table in
`../MoM binary analysis.md`; hashes are not duplicated here.

The source-shaped reconstruction is in `unitcalc.c`.

## Entry routing and identity

Overlay operand `03C8:0043` resolves to `0x98494` in all three builds: overlay body base
`0x97B30` plus thunk displacement `0x0964`. The raw far-call encoding occurs at:

| build | call-encoding file offsets |
|---|---|
| MoM 1.31 | `0x465BF`, `0x466D8`, `0x6D523`, `0x8F2A9` |
| CP 1.60 | `0x465BF`, `0x466D8`, `0x6D523`, `0x8F2A9` |
| CoM 1 | `0x465BF`, `0x466D8`, `0x6D523` |

The MoM/CP constructor pushes the unit index at `0x8F2A8`, calls this routine at `0x8F2A9`,
pops the argument at `0x8F2AE`, and stores returned `AL` as movement at `0x8F2B2`. CoM replaces
that constructor path: `0x90BE9` calls `03E0:003E`, resolved `0x9F12D`, the separately
reconstructed `Battle_Unit_Moves2` from R6.1g. The old `Unit_Moves2` body remains at its original
address in CoM.

## Operation order and build split

The byte-backed order implemented in `Unit_Moves2` is:

1. Initialize signed item movement, the item-power latch, and a 32-bit raw-effect union to zero.
2. When signed `Hero_Slot > -1`, scan exactly three equipped item words. For each item index
   greater than `-1`, latch raw item-power bit `0x00002000`, OR all item Powers into the union,
   and add signed item `moves2`.
3. Load signed unit-type `Move_Halves`, then OR unit enchantments into the same raw union.
4. Raise movement to 6 for Flight when below 6; then raise it to 4 for Chaos Channels wings when
   below 4.
5. Add 2 when raw union bit `0x00002000` is present or the item latch equals 1; then add the
   accumulated signed item movement.
6. Only for signed-positive unit-type `Transport`, scan the signed player count and calculate the
   Wind Mastery balance.

In MoM/CP, each positive signed Wind Mastery byte increments the balance when the unit owner is
that player and decrements it otherwise. Positive balance changes movement to the signed low-word
wrapped product `moves2 * 3`, divided by 2 toward zero. Negative balance divides movement by 2
toward zero.

CoM changes `0x98669` from conditional `7E 1E` to unconditional `EB 1E`. The player byte is still
read and compared, but every iteration jumps directly to the loop increment. Therefore the
balance remains zero. CoM retains the owner comparison and balance writes at `0x9866B..0x98688`,
but they are unreachable. It also patches the positive arm to double movement and the negative
arm to a no-op; both arms are unreachable because their balance predicates cannot become true.

Owner, Hero Slot, item movement, base Move Halves, Transport, Wind Mastery bytes, and unit owner
are read with the signedness stated above; unit type is explicitly zero-extended. The two 32-bit
raw-power tests use the full Borland high/low-word comparison sequences. In MoM/CP, the positive
Wind Mastery bytes are `8B C7 BA 03 00 F7 EA 99 2B C2 D1 F8 8B F8`: the low word of the signed
product is kept before `cwd; sub ax,dx; sar ax,1` performs division by two toward zero. The
negative arm uses `8B C7 99 2B C2 D1 F8 8B F8`, likewise dividing toward zero. CoM replaces those
with `8B C7`, ten NOPs, `03 F8` and `8B C7`, five NOPs, `8B F8` respectively.

## Merged findings

- The routine is the overland `Unit_Moves2`: MoM/CP use it directly in battle-unit construction.
  CoM retains the homolog but replaces that constructor call with its new battle movement routine.
- MoM 1.31 and CP 1.60 are byte-identical. CoM differs at only 17 byte positions, all in the
  Wind Mastery block.
- The exact common order is hero items, signed base Move Halves, unit enchantments, Flight floor,
  wings floor, raw/item `0x00002000` bonus, item movement, then the Transport-only Wind Mastery
  transform.
- CoM's unconditional per-player skip makes its balance stay zero. Its retained owner/balance
  block and patched double/no-op transform arms are therefore unreachable, not alternate live
  movement rules.
- The routine has no calls and no external state writes.
