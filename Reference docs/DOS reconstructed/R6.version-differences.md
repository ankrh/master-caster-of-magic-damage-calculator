# DOS reconstruction — cross-build version index and call closure

This is the merged R6.3 index for the fourteen R6.1a–h and R6.2a–f evidence artifacts, the landed
R6.5a–d call-closure reconstructions, and the two reconstructed source files, `unitcalc.c` and
`combat.c`. It has two jobs:

1. index every material 1.31 → CP 1.60 → CoM 1 split back to its owning evidence section; and
2. close the direct-call inventory for every assigned root and the calls made by its located
   first-level callees.

The three binaries match the hashes in `MoM binary analysis.md`, *The binaries*. Claude and
Codex produced independent R6.3 artifacts. At AKH's explicit direction on 2026-08-07, the
reciprocal-review round was waived and the two artifacts were merged directly. Their mechanic
inventories contain no incompatible reading. Claude's overlay-table derivation resolves the raw
far operands that the Codex artifact had conservatively left as opaque; those are stronger
byte-backed dispositions, not a mechanic disagreement.

## VROOMM overlay resolution

The executable does contain an overlay-to-file map. `tools/resolve_dos_overlays.py` decodes it:

- the MZ header gives load-module start `0x2A00` and end `0x37F80`;
- the unique `FBOV` record at `0x37F80` points to the 176-entry segment table at `0x26230`;
- inside overlay code, a far-call segment operand is eight times that table index;
- an overlaid table entry locates a resident stub, whose descriptor locates the overlay body;
- the call offset selects a five-byte `CD 3F` thunk, whose word supplies the offset in that body.

For example, `0x871BB: 9A 43 00 D0 03` selects table entry `0x03D0 / 8 = 122`,
body `0x98F60`, thunk offset `0x1627`, and therefore target `0x9A587`,
`Battle_Unit_Defense_Special`. The model predicts 32 routine starts that R6 had already fixed
independently and four overlay body ends that coincide exactly with the last assigned extent.

Resident targets also resolve: `0000:02C8 → 0x02CC8` is `abs()`,
`0000:0787 → 0x03187` is the by-value struct copier, and
`00B0:00D8 → 0x0FFA8` is the PRNG. Full byte-chain evidence is preserved in the independent
Claude artifact; the durable resolver is the executable form of that derivation.
