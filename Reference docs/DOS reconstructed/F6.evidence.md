## Result

`Apply_Chaos_Channels` draws `Random(3)-1`, giving Wings `0`, Fire Breath `1`, or Demon Skin Armor
`2`. A choice that fails its admission test becomes `-1` and is rerolled. The accepted choice
preserves the existing `_UNITS[unit_idx].mutations` byte and ORs exactly one flag:

| choice | mutation | admission in MoM 1.31 | admission in CP 1.60 | admission in CoM 1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wings | `0x08` | base type is neither Flying nor Sailing | same | base type is not Flying; Sailing test is inert |
| Fire Breath | `0x10` | signed base `ranged <= 3`, and `ranged_type` is none (`0xFF`) or Thrown (`0x64`) | signed base `ranged <= 0`, same type gate | same as CP 1.60 |
| Demon Skin Armor | `0x04` | always | always | always |

The three streams differ at only two bytes across the assigned extent:

- `0xA4E45`: `74 03` (`je 0xA4E4A`) in MoM 1.31/CP 1.60, but `EB 03`
  (`jmp 0xA4E4A`) in CoM 1. The preceding `F6 87 B4 01 02` Sailing test still executes in CoM,
  but its flags are discarded.
- `0xA4E5D`: the immediate is `03` in MoM 1.31 and `00` in CP 1.60/CoM 1. With signed `jg` bytes
  `7F 22` at `0xA4E5E`, these are the Fire Breath `ranged` ceilings above.

The `Random` dividend is zeroed by `C7 46 FC 00 00` at `0x0FFC0`; nine iterations selected by
`B9 09 00` at `0x0FFCF` shift carry bits into it with `D1 56 FC` at `0x10000` and `E2 C7` at
`0x10009`, bounding it to `0x0000..0x01FF`. The return tail at `0x10022..0x1002D` divides that
non-negative word by the positive argument and increments its `0..2` remainder. Thus `Random(3)`
is exactly `1..3`, the option is exactly `0..2`, retry terminates, and the switch default jump at
`0xA4E9A` is unreachable compiler scaffolding.

## Fields, writes, and edges

The complete address idioms bind `_UNITS` through DS:`0x9EC2`, stride `0x20`, `.type` at `+0x05`,
and `.mutations` at `+0x17`. Unit types use DS:`0x019C`, stride `0x24`, with `ranged` at `+0x03`,
`ranged_type` at `+0x04`, and `Move_Flags` at `+0x18`. The cached type is a zero-extended 16-bit
local: `26 8A 47 05; B4 00; 89 46 FE` at `0xA4E09..0xA4E12`, followed by word reloads.

The mutation reads are at `0xA4EA8`, `0xA4ECC`, and `0xA4EE0`; their OR instructions are at
`0xA4EAC`, `0xA4ED0`, and `0xA4EE4`. All arms converge on the shared address calculation at
`0xA4EAE` and the routine's sole persistent write, `26 88 47 17` at `0xA4EBA`.
