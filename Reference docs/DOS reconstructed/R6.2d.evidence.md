# R6.2d evidence — combat-resolution helpers

This is the durable evidence companion to [`combat.c`](combat.c). Claude and Codex independently
derived all twelve assigned helper extents in the three pinned DOS builds, reciprocally reviewed
them, and accepted every byte-backed correction on 2026-08-07. No disagreement survived.

The merged source is in `combat.c`. This file holds the assigned ranges, complete branch/call/write
inventories, coverage ledgers, arithmetic checks, findings, and completion counts.

## Reachability

E1–E9 enter at their first instructions. CoM's `0x9A8CA` is called only at `0x98FB5`,
`0x9AC1B` only at `0x99C00`, `0x9984B` only at `0x9A213`, and `0x9985B` only at `0x9A2CA`.
The relocated tail has one predecessor: `0x9ACFE: E9 73 EB ->0x99874`; it falls through its own
far epilogue at `0x998A0: 5F 5E 8B E5 5D CB`. The NOP hole `0x998A6..0x998C0` has no predecessor.

## Merged findings

1. `CMB_AttackRoll` uses `roll >= 8-to_hit || roll == 10` in all three builds
   (`0x98F7B..0x98F8C`). Natural 10 therefore supplies the 10% floor in every DOS version;
   sufficiently high To Hit makes every 1–10 roll succeed, establishing the 100% ceiling.
2. `CMB_DefenseRoll` has no natural-max clause. CoM's helper applies To Block only to dice 0–14,
   so defense dice beyond the fifteenth use the unmodified threshold (`0x98FB5`, `0x9A8CA`).
3. Resistance failure returns the positive margin `roll-effective`, not a Boolean
   (`0x98FF9..0x99007`). CoM narrows elemental resistance to Nature, disables Elemental Armor,
   raises Resist Elements to +4, and raises Bless to +5 (`0x990D8..0x9912D`).
4. The immunity-mask builder's 1.31 Thrown arm divides the key before comparing it with 100, so
   that arm is unreachable. CP and CoM replace the six-byte divide at `0x9922C` with NOPs and
   compare the raw key, restoring Weapon Immunity for Thrown attacks.
5. The realm classifier derives only melee (`ranged_type == 0`) from the attacker's race. Physical
   ranged and Thrown are realm-less; breath/gaze/magic keys have fixed realms. CoM alone maps Ice
   Bolt key 34 to Sorcery rather than Nature (`0x9A82F`).
6. `Range_To_Battle_Unit` returns Chebyshev distance, `max(abs(dx),abs(dy))`
   (`0x9B51A..0x9B588`). `Battle_Unit_Has_Ranged_Attack` accepts only signed keys 1–99
   (`0x9BB0C..0x9BB37`), excluding Thrown, breath, and gaze.
7. `BU_CauseFear` accepts four source forms, skips a Death-Immune target, and rolls once per target
   figure. MoM/CP use modifier 0; CoM uses -3 (`0x9BB51..0x9BC35`). Magic Immunity is not a
   direct skip here; its +30 effective-resistance bonus makes the Death-realm roll non-failing.
8. CoM's `0x9984B` helper accumulates the attack roll and retains bare-SAR
   `floor((roll-5)/2)`. `0x9985B` restores that floor only for Destruction with defense below 80
   and lower post-defense damage (`0x9984B..0x99873`).
9. CoM's relocated tail is bottom-tested: it executes its two opaque calls at least once even when
   `[0xC588] <= 0` (`0x99874..0x998A5`).
10. CoM's `SpFx`-gated melee helper grants hero mana using factor 2 or 3 times `(Level+1)`, a
    signed byte multiply followed by logical halving, and clamps to `mana_max`
    (`0x9AC1B..0x9AC8E`).
