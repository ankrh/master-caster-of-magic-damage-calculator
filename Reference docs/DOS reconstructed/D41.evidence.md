Coverage ledgers, byte inventories, build differences and dependency closure for the source-shaped

**Provenance.** Reconstructed under method 4 on 2026-08-22: two independent Codex GPT-5.6 Sol

the raw binaries and a deterministic bundle. The reviewer independently confirmed the extent,

## Extent and corrected premise

The common far routine is exactly `[0x8EAB9,0x8EDFD)`: the preceding routine's `retf` ends at
`0x8EAB8`, the prologue begins at `0x8EAB9`, this routine's `retf` is `0x8EDFC`, and the
already reconstructed constructor begins at `0x8EDFD`. VROOMM operand `03A0:0039` resolves to
this entry in all three builds. Its interface is:

```c
void __far Load_Battle_Unit(int16_t unit_idx, struct s_BATTLE_UNIT __far *bu);
```

The backlog premise said the routine copied the permanent record. The bytes falsify that wording.
The permanent `_UNITS[unit_idx]` record supplies only the type selector. The routine passes
`&unit_types[type].Melee` as the source to `_fmemcpy` and copies raw `0x24` bytes. Because
`Melee` is two bytes into a `0x24`-byte unit-type record, the final copied word comes from the
next record's name pointer and lands in `bu->Combat_Effects`; `0x8EAF5: 26 C7 47 22 00 00`
immediately clears that over-copy. The mapping is shift-by-two across the whole window, including
unit-type `Hits` to battle-unit `hits`, `Move_Flags` to `Move_Flags`, and `Sound` to
`Sound`.

The three calls are real binary/runtime dependencies, not synthetic helpers. Overlay resolution
maps `00B0:0466` to resident raw `0x10336`; its body loads the far destination from `[bp+6:8]`,
far source from `[bp+0x0A:0x0C]`, count from `[bp+0x0E]`, and the even-count arm executes
`F3 A5` (`rep movsw`) at `0x1039A` before `retf` at `0x103A3`. That independently proves the copy
interface and all 36 written bytes. `Unit_Upkeep_0x961F3` is separately located at raw `0x961F3`;
its body is not reconstructed here and the bytes do not establish which upkeep currency it returns;
`BU_Construct` is the already owned `[0x8EDFD,...)` routine. No unrepresented helper is invented
below.

## Build differences established by the body

| site | mom131 | mom160 | com1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| initialization code shape | repeated `les bx,bu` and byte stores | same as 1.31 through `0x8ECD8` | coalesced word stores and retained `es:bx` |
| `Grey_Hits` | not written | not written | set to signed sentinel `-1` at `0x8EB57` |
| Focus Magic ammunition | absent | absent | persistent `UE_FOCUS_MAGIC` floors `bu->ammo` at 4 before construction |
| hero-template ammunition | absent | absent | for a hero with valid owner, unknown ability bit `0x4` selects factor 2, else bit `0x20` selects factor 3; add `((Level+1)*factor)/4` only when copied ammo is positive |
| copied high `Move_Flags` byte | retained | retained | cleared at `0x8EC08` |
| imported `Attribs_2` high byte (`bu->Unused_1Bh`) | remains as copied | remains as copied | discarded and overwritten by unchecked signed-Level lookup in `0A 0D 11 15 1C 24 2C 37 42`; no battle-unit reader located |
| active-unit movement | no special write | no special write | if `bu` equals far pointer `[0x9226:0x9228]`, copy `_UNITS[unit_idx].moves2_max` to `movement_points` |
| zero/negative remaining figures | left as calculated | clamp to 1 figure and `hits-1` front damage | same clamp as CP |
| later animation-field zeroing | repeated far-pointer reloads | optimized after clamp, with executed NOP bridge | optimized as CoM code, with a larger executed NOP bridge |

All other semantic effects are common: type-payload copy, combat-field initialization, owner and
damage import, upkeep call, poison split, constructor call, mana fill, damage division, animation
state reset, and first-item charge import.

The Focus Magic load-bearing clue is confirmed directly. Its two branches target `0x8EBA0`, where
the saved permanent-record pointer is restored; the store is `26 C6 47 03 04` at `0x8EB9B`.
