# D34 — CoM 6.08 Supernatural binding

Method 4 package `D34`. Evidence/build scope: CoM 6.08 `WIZARDS.EXE`; calculator scope:
`com_6.08`. Two independent Codex GPT-5.6 Sol High derivations were merged, mechanically checked,
and reviewed once by Claude Opus 5 Medium. The review's four material corrections are incorporated
below. Calculator behavior is unchanged.

## Result

The proposed binding is falsified. CoM 6.08 names common attack-attribute mask `$2000`
Supernatural and `$0020` Destruction. The existing near-frame helpers
`[0x9984B,0x99857)` and `[0x9985B,0x99874)` implement a branch-specific Destruction
minimum-result floor, not Supernatural minimum damage.

The supplied source-word `$0020` anchor at `0x8F5FF` belongs to `Attribs_2`, a different field.
It projects `$4000` into melee and ranged attack attributes, ensures `Weapon_Plus1 >= 1`, and the
projected `$4000` lowers defender to-block by one at `0x99EEF..0x99EF6`.

The only located functional direct-encoding Supernatural consumer is `0x9C26D`. It gates a
material state-write path on signed
`DS:[0x9F20 + arg0 * 0x04C8] >= 240`. D34 does not bind the phase, argument identity, player-word
identity, or complete state transition. The consumer lies in newly bounded far routine
`[0x9BCE0,0x9D535)`, now reconstructed by successor [D37](D37.evidence.md).

## Distinct Supernatural consumer and successor boundary

Exact direct-encoding scans find `test byte es:[bx+0x1F],0x20` only at `0x5C96C`, `0x99D58`, and
`0x9C26D`. The first is the name binding. At `0x99D58`, immediate unconditional
`0x99D5D: EB 14` discards the flags. The functional gate is:

```text
com1:0x9C26D  26 F6 47 1F 20       test byte es:[bx+0x1F],0x20
com1:0x9C272  74 15                je 0x9C289
com1:0x9C275  B8 C8 04             mov ax,0x04C8
com1:0x9C278  F7 6E 06             imul word [bp+6]
com1:0x9C27D  81 BF 20 9F F0 00    cmp word [bx-0x60E0],0x00F0
com1:0x9C284  7D 03                jge 0x9C289
com1:0x9C286  E9 A9 FF             jmp 0x9C232
```

The accepted path reaches material writes beginning at `0x9C2B7`; the rejection flow skips them.
No broader phase or field identity is claimed.

Overlay segment `$03D8`, slot 0 resolves to `0x9BCE0`. Its natural routine has prologue
`0x9BCE0`, sole `retf` `0x9D534`, and the next slot/prologue at exclusive bound `0x9D535`.
Enumerating every `$03D8` far call and the complete stub table finds only two slot-0 callers,
`0x6C3C4` and `0x7FC98`, with no other stub landing inside. The extent contains internal
BP-sharing near returns at `0x9BE1F`, `0x9C082`, `0x9C0FB`, and `0x9C121`.

Successor [D37](D37.evidence.md) completes `[0x9BCE0,0x9D535)` in `combat.c`, including all
branches, calls, tables, state accesses, internal near helpers, caller/exit edges, the winner-player
argument, Nominal Skill word at player offset `+0x56`, candidate filters, and full state transition.

## Calculator consequence

For `com_6.08`, `floor((hits - 5) / 2)` remains unverified as Supernatural and must not be sourced
from these helpers. The bytes bind that floor to `$0020` Destruction on one resolution branch.
The calculator's currently represented Supernatural formula is left unchanged for R9-G1g to
resolve from this evidence; D34 does not implement behavior.
