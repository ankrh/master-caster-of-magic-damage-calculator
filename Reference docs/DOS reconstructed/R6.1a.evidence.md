# R6.1a — `BU_Apply_Specials`, all three DOS builds

Coverage ledgers, counts and findings for the reconstruction in
[`unitcalc.c`](./unitcalc.c). Conventions are in [README.md](./README.md).

**Provenance.** Derived independently by both agents in dual-derivation mode, then cross-reviewed
and merged: Claude 2026-08-05 (`0x8F310`–`0x8F880` and both relocated tails); Codex 2026-08-05,
independent. Two reciprocal review rounds; every entry on both sides was confirmed against the
quoted bytes and fixed. Merged by Claude 2026-08-05.

## Extent

The assigned extent was `0x8F310`–`0x8F880` in all three builds, and it is the whole routine only
in 1.31. CP 1.60 and CoM 1 replace the epilogue with a jump into space freed in the battle-unit
constructor, and those tails carry each build's only `retf`:

|---|---|---|---|---|
| `mom131` | `0x8F310`–`0x8F881` | — | — | `0x8F880` |
| `mom160` | `0x8F310`–`0x8F881` | `0x8F197`–`0x8F26E` | `jmp` at `0x8F87E` | `0x8F26D` |
| `com1` | `0x8F310`–`0x8F881` | `0x8F15C`–`0x8F233` | `jmp` at `0x8F87E` | `0x8F232` |

Both tails read the argument frame (`[bp+6]`, `[bp+0xa]`/`[bp+0xc]`, `[bp+0xe]`) rather than the
constructor's locals, so they belong to this routine. Both agents derived them and agreed on
their inclusion. Callers, from an exhaustive near-call scan of `0x88000`–`0x9F000`: the
constructor and the stat recompute, and nothing else — `0x8F2A2`/`0x90A1D` in both MoM builds,
`0x8F0E8`/`0x90743` in CoM 1.

## Findings

### The `Gold_*` / `Grey_*` accumulators are the engine's own stat-tier record

ReMoM `MOM_DAT.h` names `+0x64`–`+0x68` `Gold_Melee`, `Gold_Ranged`, `Gold_Defense`,
`Gold_Resist`, `Gold_Hits` and `+0x69`–`+0x6D` the `Grey_*` twins. Every stat *bonus* in this
routine writes the effective stat and its `Gold_*` twin in the same breath; every *reduction*
writes `Grey_*` instead, with the magnitude stored positive. The constructor zeroes `+0x24`,
`+0x25`, `+0x26` and the whole `+0x64`…`+0x6C` run at `0x8EE48`ff, so the block is per-battle
accumulator state, not roster data.

So the engine does not derive the plain/gold display split at draw time — it accumulates gold and
grey deltas alongside the effective stat as each modifier lands. Three demonstrations:

- CoM 1's Mystic Surge: `defense += 2` → `Gold_Defense += 2` (`0x8F7A3`, `0x8F7A8`) but
  `resist -= 2` → **`Grey_Resist += 2`** (`0x8F7AD`, `0x8F7B2`).
- 1.31's Berserk: `Gold_Melee += melee` *before* the doubling (`0x8F856`), and
  `Grey_Defense = defense` as a plain **assignment** before `defense = -20` (`0x8F872`,
  `0x8F879`).
- Three CoM 1 writes break the pairing and have no `Gold_*` twin: Animated's `tohit += 1`
  (`0x8F502`), Holy Armor's `toblock += 1` (`0x8F7DF`) and FocusMagic's `ranged = 3` floor
  (`0x8F84C`).

### CP 1.60 and CoM 1 moved Holy Weapon out of the constructor, so it now runs twice

In 1.31 the Holy Arms grant, the Holy Weapon to-hit bonus and the to-hit normalisation are inlined
in the **constructor** at `0x8F1A0`ff, reading its locals (`[bp-7]` mutations at `0x8F1BA`,
`[bp-4]`/`[bp-6]` enchantments at `0x8F1C0`), and run once per unit. Both later builds moved them
into `BU_Apply_Specials`, which has two callers, so they run at construction *and* at every stat
recompute.

The recompute's call passes `(unit_ench XOR bu_ench) AND bu_ench`, so a Holy Weapon held by the
unit record is filtered out of the second call's `ench` — but the Holy **Arms** grant does not
read `ench` at all, and neither does the normalisation. The normalisation is idempotent once
`melee_tohit` and `ranged_tohit` no longer share a positive minimum, so a second pass is usually
inert; the case that is not is a unit acquiring a `melee_tohit`-only bonus between the two calls.

### The to-hit normalisation has an unguarded entry path

`0x8F243` (`74 12`, `je 0x8F257`; CoM 1 `0x8F208` → `0x8F21C`) lands directly on the shared
`sub byte es:[bx+0x24],al` and runs into `add byte es:[bx+4],al`, bypassing the `cmp al,0` /
`jle` pair at `0x8F24F`/`0x8F251` (CoM 1 `0x8F214`/`0x8F216`). So a unit with `ranged_type == -1`
always has `melee_tohit` driven to 0 and that amount folded into base `tohit` — **including when
`melee_tohit` is negative, which lowers `tohit`**. The `jle` guard governs the
`ranged_type != -1` path only, where it skips all three writes.

Both derivations initially rendered this as a second `if (common > 0)` guard, which suppresses the
negative case; it was caught in review and corrected in both.

grants `Attribs_1 |= 0x0100`, Weapon Immunity, exactly as the helptext says. That is an
independent check that `+0x19` is `Attribs_1`'s high byte.

Land Link's ranged gate is `cmp ranged_type,0x64 / jle skip` — strictly **greater than 100**, so
breath (101–102) and gaze (103–105) qualify and Thrown (100) does not. It matches the helptext's
"breath attacks" wording. FocusMagic's second arm (`0x8F839`, `jg`) selects the same band on the
same field; those two are the only sites in the routine that do.

FocusMagic's `base_rt` compare is **signed** (`0x8F827` is `7C 10`, `jl`), and the unit-type
table's `ranged_type` column does contain `0xFF` — it is that table's "no ranged attack" encoding
and the majority value across types 0–200. So a melee-only base type takes the fallback arm, not
`ranged += 3`. Reading the column as unsigned inverts the outcome for exactly the units the
spell's headline effect is about.

### FocusMagic's `ammo > 0` gate is vacuous: CoM 1 floors the unit's ammunition at 4 first

`0x8F7FB` skips the whole ranged half when `bu->ammo <= 0`, but no Focus Magic unit can reach it
with ammo at zero. The battle-unit setup routine at `0x8EAB9` — outside this item's extent and
R6.1b's constructor extent, and now fully reconstructed by [D41](./D41.evidence.md) — carries a
CoM 1-only block just before its call to the constructor at `0x8EC99`:

```
8EB78  c41ec29e      les  bx,[0x9EC2]              ; _UNITS[], stride 0x20
8EB87  26f747180008  test word es:[bx+18h],0800h   ; _UNITS[si].enchantments & UE_FOCUS_MAGIC
8EB8F  c45e08        les  bx,[bp+08h]              ; switch to the BATTLE_UNIT (flags survive)
8EB92  740c          jz   8EBA0
8EB94  26807f0304    cmp  byte es:[bx+03h],4       ; bu->ammo
8EB99  7f05          jg   8EBA0
8EB9B  26c6470304    mov  byte es:[bx+03h],4       ; bu->ammo = 4
```

i.e. `if (_UNITS[si].enchantments & UE_FOCUS_MAGIC) bu->ammo = max(bu->ammo, 4);`. The six-byte
`test` pattern occurs **once** in `com1` and **not at all** in `mom131` or `cp160`, and no other
write to `bu->ammo` lies between `0x8EB9B` and the read at `0x8F7FB`. So the answer to the posed
alternative is the second one — the setup seeds it non-zero — and the signed `base_rt` fallback is
reachable for every attack-less, missile or boulder CoM 1 unit that carries the enchantment:
140 of the 192 records in the shipped roster.

The block reads the **persistent** enchantment word, not the battle unit's, so a mid-combat cast
would not raise ammunition. The prose disagrees with the binary in two places, and the binary
wins: `CoM1manual.HTML` says the created attack carries **3** ammo where `0x8EB9B` writes **4**,
and helptext `:2176` describes only the magical-ranged arms, naming neither the breaths nor the
gazes that `0x8F825`'s band admits.

### CoM 1's Holy Armor is the DOS ancestor of the modern `Defense > 5` test

`0x8F7C1`: `if (defense <= 5) { defense += 2; Gold_Defense += 2; } else toblock += 1;` against
helptext `:875` — "receives a +1 To Defend bonus. However, units that have 5 or lower defense,
receive 2 defense instead." So `toblock` (`+0x26`) is the "To Defend" stat, and the threshold reads
the **running** `defense` at this point in the sequence. Both MoM builds have the unconditional
`+2` at `0x8F7E7`.

### Black Channels' ranged gate is `!= -1`, not `!= 0`

`0x8F437` (CoM 1 `0x8F4EB`) tests `ranged_type == 0xFF`. `-1` is the "ranged attack spent or
disabled" sentinel, written at `0x96DFC`, `0x9B0D5`, `0x9B135`, `0x9B38C` and `0x9B3EC`; `0` is
the separate "unit has no ranged attack" value. So Black Channels / Animated adds `+1 ranged` and
`+1 Gold_Ranged` to a unit with no ranged attack at all. The strength is inert in combat, since
`Has_Ranged_Attack` (`0x9BB03`) requires `0 < ranged_type < 100`, but the accumulator is not.

### Smaller version differences

- **Iron Skin and Stone Skin are mutually exclusive in MoM.** `0x8F4F9` is `jmp 0x8F52C`, so a
  unit holding both gets `+5`, never `+6`. CoM 1 has no else-arm and repurposed the slot.
- **CoM 1's Wraith Form grants the magic-weapon upgrade** (`0x8F36B`/`0x8F372`), matching helptext
  `:2158`; neither MoM build has the store.
- **CoM 1 adds `Abilities |= 1` to all three Chaos-Channels mutation blocks** (`0x8F469`,
  `0x8F486`, `0x8F751`).
- **The fire-breath mutation writes `ranged = 4` in CoM 1** (`0x8F47C`) against `2` in both MoM
  builds (`0x8F728`), and the write is an assignment, not an add.
- **CoM 1's Holy Arms grant drops CP 1.60's two undead exclusions** and adds a unit-type ceiling
  (`0x8F172`, `73 1E`, `jae` — the only unsigned comparison anywhere in the item).

### A routine that is not `BU_Apply_Specials` lies inside CoM 1's extent

`0x8F379`–`0x8F3A6` is a **near** routine (`ret`, not `retf`) called from the battle-unit
constructor at `0x8EF5F`, sharing that frame's `si` and `[bp+6]`. `BU_Apply_Specials` jumps over
it at `0x8F377` and never reaches it. R6.1b establishes that its `cs:0x17D7` table is raw
`0x8F3A7` bytes `B1 18 3E 0E C3 23`, overriding mana for Angel (24), Apprentices (14), and
Djinn (35); see `R6.1b.evidence.md`, *CoM 1 adds a three-entry mana override table*.

### The `0x97` ceiling is the normal/fantastic cut, one row low (Q18, resolved 2026-09-01)

Every consumer compares a **base unit type** against `0x97` and **partitions strictly at** it —
`{0x00..0x96}` against `{0x97..0xFF}`, never equality-inclusive. The type is the index into the
`0x24`-stride unit-type table at `DS:0x019C`; surrounding code reaches that table's own fields
through it (`imul 0x24` then `[bx+0x19C]` name, `+0x1A6` cost, `+0x1A9` race, `+0x1BA`
`Abilities`). Which arm is the *interesting* one is local, so "admits" is not safe as a universal
reading: at `0xF4789` the `< 0x97` arm falls through to an unconditional `jmp 0xF4828`, and at
`0xE8973` the `jb` arm is the candidate-skip path.

#### One patched byte proves what the constant is

`0x5C269` settles the meaning without any table decoding. The bytes `[0x5C240,0x5C274)` are
**identical in all three builds except one**:

| Build | `0x5C269` |
|---|---|
| MoM 1.31 | `81 7E EC` **`9A`** `00` — `cmp word [bp-0x14], 0x9A` |
| MoM CP 1.60 | `81 7E EC` **`9A`** `00` |
| CoM 1 | `81 7E EC` **`97`** `00` |

The producer four instructions earlier is unambiguous: `mov ax,[bp+6]` / `shl ax,5` /
`les bx,[0x9EC2]` / `mov al,es:[bx+5]` / `mov ah,0` / `mov [bp-0x14],ax`. Same gate, same routine,
one immediate patched. And `0x9A` is exactly MoM 1.31's first fantastic row (`Magic Spirit`;
1.31's `0x98`/`0x99` are `War Trolls`/`War Mammoths`, race `0x0D`), so **in 1.31 the gate is
precisely "normal unit"**. CoM 1 moved the table's first fantastic row down to `0x98` (`Boars`)
and moved the constant to `0x97` — one further than the table moved. The off-by-one is therefore
demonstrated, not inferred: the constant was correct before, and the retarget undershot.

This also explains the wrong D37 name. `UNITTYPE_MAGIC_SPIRIT_BOUND` is an accurate name for the
**1.31** constant `0x9A`; it is wrong for CoM 1's `0x97`, where Magic Spirit sits at `0x9A`.

**Correction to a claim previously made here: the ceiling is not a CoM 1 addition.** MoM 1.31
carries 11 sites comparing a base unit type against `0x9A`, and CP 1.60 carries 11 (nine in the
`es:[bx+5|7]` form plus two `cmp al,0x9A` at `0xE191A`/`0xE19B3`). CoM 1 has **zero** compares
against `0x9A`. What CoM 1 did was retarget the value and roughly double the site count:

| | inherited at the same file offset, value patched | new in CoM 1 |
|---|---|---|
| sites | `0x5C269`, `0x7358E`, `0x7365A`, `0x977EF`, `0x9D27B`, `0x9D290` (from 1.31), plus `0xE8805`, `0xE8973` (from CP 1.60's layout) | the other 16 |

the strict partition a safe universal claim even though the useful arm varies.

Three forms deviate from the common one, none of them moving the partition:

- **Container.** `0x7358E`, `0x7365A`, `0x9D27B` and `0x9D290` read the **lair record**
  (`DS:[0x9CC0]`, stride `0x18`) at `+0x05` and `+0x07` — the two guard unit-type slots — not a
  `_UNITS` record. Same value space, different structure. Independent binding: `0x9C9F4` loads
  `_UNITS[].type` and compares it against lair `+0x05` at `0x9CA09`, and `0x9CA6C`/`0x9CA81` do
  the same against `+0x07`.
- **Width and signedness.** `0x97656` **and `0x5C269`** are word compares with a *signed* `jl`,
  both built the same safe way — `mov al,es:[bx+5]`, `mov ah,0`, then compare the word. `ah` is
  zeroed and no branch target bypasses the zeroing, so the value is `0..255` and `jl` partitions
  exactly as `jb`. The other 22 sites compare a byte with `jae`/`jb`.
- **Register.** `0x8F170` and the three `0xE34xx` sites use `mov al,es:[bx+5]` then `cmp al,0x97`;
  `0xF4789` carries the type in `ch` across ~0x4C bytes before comparing.

The four sites inside this item's and its siblings' extents, with their local effect:

| Site | Compare | Branch | Effect of `type < 0x97` |
|---|---|---|---|
| Holy Arms, `0x8F16C`–`0x8F172` | `3C 97` on `al` | `73 1E jae` skips | grant runs |
| Heavenly Light, `0x905E7` | `26 80 7F 05 97` | `72 02 jb` keeps | `cl` keeps `_UNITS[].mutations`; otherwise `cl = 1` (Magic Weapons), and `test cl,3` then withholds the melee/ranged To Hit increments only — the defense/resist/`Weapon_Plus1` writes are outside the gate |

| Heroism absent, `0x8F8E3` | `26 80 7F 05 97` | `72 08 jb` skips | no write; `>= 0x97` instead persists `Level = 0` |

The unit-type table's normal/fantastic boundary is at `0x98` — see *CoM 1 unit-type table extent
and realm boundary* in `MoM binary analysis.md` for the row decode and the roster alignment. So
`type < 0x97` admits heroes, ships and every normal-race row **except** `0x97`, the last of the six
`Settlers` rows, and excludes every fantastic row from `0x98` (`Boars`, Nature) up. Helptext
`:1074` — "affects units who base unit type is a normal unit" — is therefore implemented, with the
cut placed one row below the first fantastic row. The four summons the open question feared are at
`0x99` Fire Elemental, `0x9A` Magic Spirit, `0x9B` Hell Hounds, `0x9C` Gargoyles and are all
excluded; that reading came from applying the `units_mom.js` `id = index + 1` convention, which is
not the CoM 1 index space. The experience site corroborates the rule independently: only
`type < 0x97` earns experience, and fantastic creatures do not gain levels.

Whether type `0x97` is reachable in CoM 1 is not settled here. CoM 1 rewrote its race byte from
1.31's `0x0D` (Troll) to `0x00`, and five of the six `Settlers` rows carry race `0x00`, so the race
field is no longer what selects a race's settler row. It does not change the answer: `Settlers` has
no melee or ranged strength, so both weapon-quality consumers are inert on it.

**Scope.** The rule is one predicate reused across the engine, but only **four of the 24** sites
are inside an attack: Holy Arms, Heavenly Light and the two Heroism writes. The rest are overland
(lair guard creation, `Calc_Unit_Level`, per-player `_UNITS` scans, the `0x5C269` information
formatter), post-combat, or spell-targeting and AI valuation — all outside the calculator's scope
per `CLAUDE.md`, *Out of scope*. Breadth of the census does not widen what a combat model has to
implement.

Over the 192 units in `Calculator/units_com.js`, `templateId < 0x97` and `!baseFantastic` agree
exactly (highest normal 150, lowest fantastic 152, and 151 is not in the roster), so for roster
units the gate reduces to the fantastic flag. The agreement is not a coincidence of two labels:
`tools/parse_tweaker_unit_data.py:295-317` sets `templateId` to the Tweaker `Nr`, which *is* the
binary index, while assigning the picker `id` independently — the two diverge precisely because
the six `Settlers` rows are excluded at `:432-448`. So `templateId` tracks `_UNITS[].+0x05` and the
picker `id` does not, and `baseFantastic` is the type record's own Fantastic flag carried through
the export rather than an editorial label. A custom unit has no `templateId`, so the flag is the
only discriminator available there.
