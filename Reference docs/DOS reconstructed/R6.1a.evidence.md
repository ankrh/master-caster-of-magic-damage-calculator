# R6.1a — `BU_Apply_Specials`, all three DOS builds

Coverage ledgers, counts and findings for the reconstruction in
[`unitcalc.c`](./unitcalc.c). Conventions are in [README.md](./README.md).

**Provenance.** Derived independently by both agents in dual-derivation mode, then cross-reviewed
and merged: Claude 2026-08-05 (`0x8F310`–`0x8F880` and both relocated tails); Codex 2026-08-05,
independent. Two reciprocal review rounds; every entry on both sides was confirmed against the
quoted bytes and fixed. Merged by Claude 2026-08-05.

## Extent

The assigned extent was `0x8F310`–`0x8F880` in all three builds, and it is the whole routine only
in 1.31. CP 1.60 and CoM 1 replace the epilogue with a jump into space freed in the battle-unit
constructor, and those tails carry each build's only `retf`:

| Build | Main body | Tail | Reached by | `retf` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `mom131` | `0x8F310`–`0x8F881` | — | — | `0x8F880` |
| `mom160` | `0x8F310`–`0x8F881` | `0x8F197`–`0x8F26E` | `jmp` at `0x8F87E` | `0x8F26D` |
| `com1` | `0x8F310`–`0x8F881` | `0x8F15C`–`0x8F233` | `jmp` at `0x8F87E` | `0x8F232` |

Both tails read the argument frame (`[bp+6]`, `[bp+0xa]`/`[bp+0xc]`, `[bp+0xe]`) rather than the
constructor's locals, so they belong to this routine. Both agents derived them and agreed on
their inclusion. Callers, from an exhaustive near-call scan of `0x88000`–`0x9F000`: the
constructor and the stat recompute, and nothing else — `0x8F2A2`/`0x90A1D` in both MoM builds,
`0x8F0E8`/`0x90743` in CoM 1.

## Findings

### The `Gold_*` / `Grey_*` accumulators are the engine's own stat-tier record

ReMoM `MOM_DAT.h` names `+0x64`–`+0x68` `Gold_Melee`, `Gold_Ranged`, `Gold_Defense`,
`Gold_Resist`, `Gold_Hits` and `+0x69`–`+0x6D` the `Grey_*` twins. Every stat *bonus* in this
routine writes the effective stat and its `Gold_*` twin in the same breath; every *reduction*
writes `Grey_*` instead, with the magnitude stored positive. The constructor zeroes `+0x24`,
`+0x25`, `+0x26` and the whole `+0x64`…`+0x6C` run at `0x8EE48`ff, so the block is per-battle
accumulator state, not roster data.

So the engine does not derive the plain/gold display split at draw time — it accumulates gold and
grey deltas alongside the effective stat as each modifier lands. Three demonstrations:

- CoM 1's Mystic Surge: `defense += 2` → `Gold_Defense += 2` (`0x8F7A3`, `0x8F7A8`) but
  `resist -= 2` → **`Grey_Resist += 2`** (`0x8F7AD`, `0x8F7B2`).
- 1.31's Berserk: `Gold_Melee += melee` *before* the doubling (`0x8F856`), and
  `Grey_Defense = defense` as a plain **assignment** before `defense = -20` (`0x8F872`,
  `0x8F879`).
- Three CoM 1 writes break the pairing and have no `Gold_*` twin: Animated's `tohit += 1`
  (`0x8F502`), Holy Armor's `toblock += 1` (`0x8F7DF`) and FocusMagic's `ranged = 3` floor
  (`0x8F84C`).

### CP 1.60 and CoM 1 moved Holy Weapon out of the constructor, so it now runs twice

In 1.31 the Holy Arms grant, the Holy Weapon to-hit bonus and the to-hit normalisation are inlined
in the **constructor** at `0x8F1A0`ff, reading its locals (`[bp-7]` mutations at `0x8F1BA`,
`[bp-4]`/`[bp-6]` enchantments at `0x8F1C0`), and run once per unit. Both later builds moved them
into `BU_Apply_Specials`, which has two callers, so they run at construction *and* at every stat
recompute.

The recompute's call passes `(unit_ench XOR bu_ench) AND bu_ench`, so a Holy Weapon held by the
unit record is filtered out of the second call's `ench` — but the Holy **Arms** grant does not
read `ench` at all, and neither does the normalisation. The normalisation is idempotent once
`melee_tohit` and `ranged_tohit` no longer share a positive minimum, so a second pass is usually
inert; the case that is not is a unit acquiring a `melee_tohit`-only bonus between the two calls.

### The to-hit normalisation has an unguarded entry path

`0x8F243` (`74 12`, `je 0x8F257`; CoM 1 `0x8F208` → `0x8F21C`) lands directly on the shared
`sub byte es:[bx+0x24],al` and runs into `add byte es:[bx+4],al`, bypassing the `cmp al,0` /
`jle` pair at `0x8F24F`/`0x8F251` (CoM 1 `0x8F214`/`0x8F216`). So a unit with `ranged_type == -1`
always has `melee_tohit` driven to 0 and that amount folded into base `tohit` — **including when
`melee_tohit` is negative, which lowers `tohit`**. The `jle` guard governs the
`ranged_type != -1` path only, where it skips all three writes.

Both derivations initially rendered this as a second `if (common > 0)` guard, which suppresses the
negative case; it was caught in review and corrected in both.

Dead* spell (`:1357`) grants the `0x10` Animated enchantment; `0x04` is the Blood Lust curse.

`0x8F506` is `26 80 4f 19 01`, a write to `+0x19` — the **high** byte of `Attribs_1` — so Animated
grants `Attribs_1 |= 0x0100`, Weapon Immunity, exactly as the helptext says. That is an
independent check that `+0x19` is `Attribs_1`'s high byte.

### CoM 1 gained three blocks with no MoM counterpart

| Slot | Name | Site | Effect | Helptext |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `0x00002000` | Endurance | `0x8F439` | `defense += 2` | `:854` |
| `0x00000080` | Land Link | `0x8F75C` | if `race >= 0x0F`: `melee += 2`, `defense += 2`, and `ranged += 2` when `ranged_type > 100`; then `Move_Flags \|= 0x60` unconditionally | `:106` |
| `0x00000800` | FocusMagic | `0x8F7E6` | `mana_max += 15` if non-zero, then the magical-ranged conversion | `:321`, `:2176` |

Land Link's ranged gate is `cmp ranged_type,0x64 / jle skip` — strictly **greater than 100**, so
breath (101–102) and gaze (103–105) qualify and Thrown (100) does not. It is the only site in the
routine that selects that band, and it matches the helptext's "breath attacks" wording.

FocusMagic's `base_rt` compare is **signed** (`0x8F827` is `7C 10`, `jl`), and the unit-type
table's `ranged_type` column does contain `0xFF` — it is that table's "no ranged attack" encoding
and the majority value across types 0–200. So a melee-only base type takes the fallback arm, not
`ranged += 3`. Reading the column as unsigned inverts the outcome for exactly the units the
spell's headline effect is about.

### CoM 1's Holy Armor is the DOS ancestor of the modern `Defense > 5` test

`0x8F7C1`: `if (defense <= 5) { defense += 2; Gold_Defense += 2; } else toblock += 1;` against
helptext `:875` — "receives a +1 To Defend bonus. However, units that have 5 or lower defense,
receive 2 defense instead." So `toblock` (`+0x26`) is the "To Defend" stat, and the threshold reads
the **running** `defense` at this point in the sequence. Both MoM builds have the unconditional
`+2` at `0x8F7E7`.

### Black Channels' ranged gate is `!= -1`, not `!= 0`

`0x8F437` (CoM 1 `0x8F4EB`) tests `ranged_type == 0xFF`. `-1` is the "ranged attack spent or
disabled" sentinel, written at `0x96DFC`, `0x9B0D5`, `0x9B135`, `0x9B38C` and `0x9B3EC`; `0` is
the separate "unit has no ranged attack" value. So Black Channels / Animated adds `+1 ranged` and
`+1 Gold_Ranged` to a unit with no ranged attack at all. The strength is inert in combat, since
`Has_Ranged_Attack` (`0x9BB03`) requires `0 < ranged_type < 100`, but the accumulator is not.

### Smaller version differences

- **Iron Skin and Stone Skin are mutually exclusive in MoM.** `0x8F4F9` is `jmp 0x8F52C`, so a
  unit holding both gets `+5`, never `+6`. CoM 1 has no else-arm and repurposed the slot.
- **CoM 1's Wraith Form grants the magic-weapon upgrade** (`0x8F36B`/`0x8F372`), matching helptext
  `:2158`; neither MoM build has the store.
- **CoM 1 adds `Abilities |= 1` to all three Chaos-Channels mutation blocks** (`0x8F469`,
  `0x8F486`, `0x8F751`).
- **The fire-breath mutation writes `ranged = 4` in CoM 1** (`0x8F47C`) against `2` in both MoM
  builds (`0x8F728`), and the write is an assignment, not an add.
- **CoM 1's Holy Arms grant drops CP 1.60's two undead exclusions** and adds a unit-type ceiling
  (`0x8F172`, `73 1E`, `jae` — the only unsigned comparison anywhere in the item).

### A routine that is not `BU_Apply_Specials` lies inside CoM 1's extent

`0x8F379`–`0x8F3A6` is a **near** routine (`ret`, not `retf`) called from the battle-unit
constructor at `0x8EF5F`, sharing that frame's `si` and `[bp+6]`. `BU_Apply_Specials` jumps over
it at `0x8F377` and never reaches it. R6.1b establishes that its `cs:0x17D7` table is raw
`0x8F3A7` bytes `B1 18 3E 0E C3 23`, overriding mana for Angel (24), Apprentices (14), and
Djinn (35); see `R6.1b.evidence.md`, *CoM 1 adds a three-entry mana override table*.

## Open questions

Stated here with their evidence; `Calculator/BACKLOG.md` is the authority for whether they are
actionable.

- **FocusMagic's `ammo > 0` gate.** `0x8F7FB` skips the entire ranged half when `bu->ammo <= 0`,
  yet helptext `:2176` says the unit "gains a strength 3 magical ranged attack" unconditionally. A
  unit with no ranged attack would normally have `ammo == 0`, which would make the headline effect
  unreachable — and in the unit-type table the types whose `ranged_type` is `0xFF` also carry
  `+3 == 0`. Either `+0x03` is not `ammo` in CoM 1, or the constructor seeds it non-zero, or the
  helptext overstates the spell. This also decides whether the signed `base_rt` path above is
  reachable in play.
- **What CoM 1's Holy Arms unit-type ceiling of `0x97` selects.** Helptext `:1074` says Holy Arms
  "affects units who base unit type is a normal unit", which a type ceiling could implement — but
  if the MoM index↔roster-id mapping (roster id = record index + 1) carries over to CoM 1, the
  ceiling falls between Gargoyles and Fire Giant, admitting Fire Elemental, Magic Spirit, Hell
  Hounds and Gargoyles, which are not normal units. Either the mapping does not carry over or the
  gate is not the helptext's rule.

## Disputed

None. Both derivations agree statement for statement after two review rounds.

One correction was applied at merge rather than in review: at `com1 0x8F34A` the bytes are
`A9 02 00` (`test ax,0x0002`), and `ax` is the high word, so the mask is `0x00020000` (Flight).
Codex's derivation carried `0x00000002` there — the same half-swap it had corrected at the
homologous 1.31 site `0x8F380` — because the review entry that enumerated those swaps did not list
this site. The bytes are quoted identically by both derivations, so the reading is settled by them
rather than arbitrated; the guard-mask table above exists so that a swap is checkable in one place
instead of site by site.
