## Destination and time are separate

A battle record carries both cast-state inputs and calculated fields. [D41](D41.evidence.md)
`Load_Battle_Unit` clears Combat_Effects at `0x8EAF5` in all builds. Later A32 can write that field;
R6.1d reads it while recomputing battle stats. These are separate engine operations. This
establishes the location and local ordering of the known reads/writes, not a universal recalc
after every cast, persistence after battle, or permission to place all casts before construction.

True Sight's second Specials pass receives battle enchantments absent from the permanent mask
(`(~permanent) & battle`) at 131/160 `[909CB,90A23)`, C1 `[906F4,90749)`. Its earlier constructor
pass and item source also exist in unitcalc.c. A consumer fed from several sources cannot prove
which source a direct cast wrote. F277.1 and the census's P8.1 retain their caller-schedule scope.

## What still needs evidence, and what does not

- **Citation/implementation:** reuse A32 for Shatter and the generic battle store. Keep these
  permanent-record calculator steps UNVERIFIED. F279.2 owns record design; F279.4 sizes subsequent
  migrations. No new arithmetic reconstruction is needed for any of these nine consumers.
- **Interpretation/binding:** establish named class-13 runtime records where needed; retain the
  distinction between an actual player admission path, a resistance mechanism, and AI selection.
  Full admission/recast/clear/timing remains with P3.1 (True Sight), P3.3 (battle curses), P3.4
  (Warp/mental curses); Illusion refusal stays F250.4.
- **Missing reconstructed writer:** Mind Storm class-14 `[82451,8262D)`, True Sight combat
  class-1/15 `[8262D,8277B)`, Warp dispatch `[82D59,82E53)` plus helper `[AE020,AE126)` are retained
  preflight leads, not new findings of this pass. Before deriving, reconfirm coverage, entry
  binding and packet size in all builds. Overland True Sight remains unlocated/unbounded.

The pending class-13/16 packet extends A32 through `0x82451`. Its additional code is unnecessary
to re-establish the generic `Combat_Effects` store. Its potential value is immediate Black Sleep
side effects and post-store calls/version differences, relevant only when a downstream question
requires those facts. The unmerged derivations do not settle opaque callees or complete timing.
Retain them unchanged; do not merge them without the required successful revision/review, and do
not launch that continuation simply because it was previously prepared.
