callees: `BU_Apply_Hero_Items` at `0x8DBD0..0x8E039` in every build, and `BU_HitPoints` at
`0x8E668..0x8E850` in MoM 1.31/CP 1.60 and `0x8E668..0x8E7AA` in CoM 1. Extents are half-open

## Findings

### Later builds turn the item block into a patch surface

MoM 1.31 applies item Defense, movement and Resistance by four ordinary reload/add sequences.
CP 1.60 compacts them, adds Merging to `Move_Flags`, and carries three proposed item-power gates
whose branches are patched from `je` to unconditional `jmp` at `0x8DD17`, `0x8DD3C` and
`0x8DD4D`. CoM restores those three conditional branches but removes the ordinary item movement
add (`0x8DD27..0x8DD2B`). Its raw low-word `0x2000` grants Teleport movement, high-word `0x0800`
grants Fire/Cold Immunity plus Immolation, and high-word `0x0100` grants Death Immunity plus Lucky
(`0x8DD11..0x8DD59`).

The CoM manual names these repurposed powers Teleportation, Inner Fire and Divine Protection and
says they replace Endurance and Giant Strength. The executable effects agree. The shipped CoM
helptext instead retains Endurance and Giant Strength and names none of the three replacements;
that co-equal prose conflict is Q21.

### CoM removes both old Giant Strength attack blocks

MoM item Giant Strength adds one melee and Gold Melee, and also one ranged and Gold Ranged for an
Axe feeding raw Thrown (`0x8DE04..0x8DE47`, `0x8DF94..0x8DFF7`). CoM NOP-fills both complete
blocks. Its manual nevertheless says replacement power Inner Fire grants “+1 attack.” No write in
either assigned routine restores that point. The adjacent item-enchantment helper at `0x8E039`,
called from `0x8DC8A`, is outside scope and remains the untraced candidate recorded as Q22.

### Item eligibility is weapon- and attack-class-specific

Sword/Mace/Axe and Misc items feed melee. Bow feeds missile ranged in both MoM builds, while CoM
widens the signed decade test to every class `<= 3`; Staff and Wand feed magical ranged, Axe feeds
raw Thrown, and Misc feeds every ranged type (`0x8DD5C..0x8DF07`). Flaming adds three to both the
ordinary and Gold accumulator for every eligible channel. The four positive `jg` edges at
`0x8DDE0`, `0x8DE23`, `0x8DF6E` and `0x8DFB3` are live; only their preceding masked-negative
`jl` edges are dead.

### CP and CoM add a second hit-point return and item Lion Heart scan

MoM 1.31 returns only hits in AX. CP and CoM also return the corresponding Gold Hits contribution
in DX, consumed by the constructor as DL (`0x8E846..0x8E848`, CoM `0x8E7A0..0x8E7A2`). CP scans
the hero's three items for Lion Heart unless its gold accumulator already exceeds one; a found
power grants flat +3 hits/gold. CoM scans only while that accumulator is zero and calls an embedded
helper that adds unsigned `8 / unit_type.Figures` to both totals (`0x8E6A0..0x8E6C1`). Persistent
Lion Heart uses the same helper in CoM, while MoM uses flat +3.

### CoM replaces MoM's level ladder for heroes

MoM applies Heroism's minimum level 3, a first hero step, Constitution and Super Constitution,
then hero/shared threshold increments through level 8 (`0x8E685..0x8E80D`). CoM has no Heroism
test. A hero receives its entire signed level in one add before the two Constitution abilities;
a nonhero gets only the `level > 2` and `level > 4` steps (`0x8E6CF..0x8E767`). The Super
Constitution arithmetic is signed `((level + 1) * 3) / 2` with Borland's `cwd/sub/sar`
truncate-toward-zero correction in every build.

### Charm of Life differs on negative intermediate values

Every build adds signed `hits / 4` when Charm of Life is active. MoM 1.31 assigns a minimum of one
with `C7 46 FE 01 00` at `0x8E83E`. CP and CoM instead increment the quotient once with
`FF 46 FE` at `0x8E83D` / `0x8E797`. The forms agree for ordinary nonnegative hit totals but
diverge if the intermediate is negative; the later builds also add that same quotient to Gold
Hits.

### Two non-code ranges remain load-bearing evidence

CoM's nine bytes at `0x8DD5B..0x8DD64` are jumped over as code but are a live CS-relative lookup
table read at `0x8EC12`. CP's `0x8E796..0x8E7C7` is the opposite: unreachable 1.31 lookup residue
whose first byte is no longer instruction-aligned. CP's `0x8E794 EB 31` jumps directly to the
live `0x8E7C7 A9 00 20` Super Constitution test.

## Open questions

- **Q21 — CoM item-power names.** The manual explicitly replaces Endurance/Giant Strength with
  Teleportation/Inner Fire and adds Divine Protection; `Reference docs/CoM helptext.txt` retains
  the old names and descriptions and contains none of the replacements. The binary effects match
  the manual, but the shipped prose sources remain contradictory.
- **Q22 — Inner Fire's +1 attack.** The manual promises it, but CoM NOP-fills the old Giant
  Strength melee/ranged writes and neither assigned routine adds a replacement. Trace the real
  adjacent callee `0x8E039`, reached at `com1:0x8DC8A`, before deciding whether the executable
  implements the point elsewhere.
