## Result and source reconciliation

All three builds' class-14 body writes `BATTLE_UNIT.Combat_Effects` at `0x82520`. Classes 1/15
write battle enchantment words `+0x3C` then `+0x3A` at `0x826B1`/`0x826B6`. These fields belong
to the battle carrier; the ordinary stores here do not target the permanent `_UNITS` record.
The mapping reuses [A32](A32.evidence.md) and [unitcalc.c](unitcalc.c)'s field/consumer annotations:
`+0x18` is Attribs_1 with Magic/Illusion masks `0x20`/`0x08`; `+0x22` is Combat_Effects;
`+0x3A/+0x3C` are its enchantment words. This mapping was not an input to either cold derivation.

Raw Mind Storm record 67 supplies class14, Sorcery byte1, low mask8 in all builds. Under the
explicit runtime-loading/index assumptions, the local body refuses Magic Immunity and then
Sorcery/Illusion Immunity before its first call/store. This is direct class14 resolution evidence,
not the A32 AI heuristic. It narrows F250.4's DOS Mind Storm question; it does not establish
Vertigo's different class13 admission or close runtime binding. The saved aggregate guard is
skipped for this record; its provenance remains outside the packet.

Raw True Sight record131 supplies class1, low mask0/high mask0x40. Under the same conditions and
post-call record/index stability, the ordinary two stores set battle True Sight, including the
literal zero-mask low-word store. Class1/15 has no local refusal branch before its first opaque
call. That absence within this island is not a complete target-admission result.

## Scope and limits

Only the missing direct writer islands were reconstructed. The reused dispatcher is 39 of the
1148 instructions; remaining counts include patch NOPs. Initial totals 307/315/526 for
131/160/C1 are within1200; no expansion or new evidence was supplied. Known consumers and
class13/16 store were excluded. Warp and overland True Sight remain outside this subunit.

| Build | Start | End | Disposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| all three | `0x81F4C` | `0x81F6F` | existing A32 dispatcher, reused context |
| all three | `0x82451` | `0x8262D` | reconstructed class14 island |
| all three | `0x8262D` | `0x8277B` | reconstructed class1/15 island |

The source uses C-like register/frame notation, not a recovered callable signature or an emulator.
`BADDR`, `SADDR`, `BARG`, `CALL6` expand the displayed machine sequences; they are not invented
game helper calls. Whole-state opaque call transitions preserve uncertainty about returned
registers/frame/global memory. Presentation-only BUILD/TAIL selections expose version differences.
This keeps exact stack-carried values and pointer reload/reuse visible while caller/callee ABI
contracts remain outside scope. No claim is made that these fragments compile as a standalone C program.

The raw packet proves record routing only conditionally: runtime LBX loading, entry-index meaning,
callee behavior, saved-input provenance and later persistence are not reconstructed. No permanent
cast provenance is promoted. See [reconciliation](F250.3.reconciliation.md) for remaining claims.
