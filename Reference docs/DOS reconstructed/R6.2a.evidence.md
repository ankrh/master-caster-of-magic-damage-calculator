# R6.2a evidence — `BU_AttackTarget`

This is the durable evidence companion to [`combat.c`](combat.c). Claude and Codex derived the
routine independently on 2026-08-06, reciprocally reviewed the two derivations, and accepted all
review corrections. Codex merged the union on 2026-08-07. No disagreement survived review.

CoM 1's `0x99849: jmp 0x998C1` skips the R6.2d island at `[0x9984B,0x998C1)`; that island is
not part of `BU_AttackTarget`. The two unresolved overlay callees at `0x992CF` and `0x99670` are
kept under their binary names in the C. Their descriptive `Check_Attack_Ranged` and
`Check_Wall_Of_Fire_Attack` attributions come from ReMoM and are orientation, not binary proof.
The routine indexes the battle-unit array through the global base pointer at `0x692A`; the merged
C uses the established `_battle_units[]` vocabulary.

## Merged findings

1. Ranged eligibility is `ranged_type > 0` plus the overlay check. Failure writes the observable
   sentinel by adding `-5` to each of the three defender buckets (`0x992F4`, `0x992FD`,
   `0x99473..0x99487`) and returns.
2. MoM 1.31's Haste repeat-shot mana classification divides ranged type by ten but then reuses
   stale `SI == 3` as a battle-unit index. Slot 3's magic/caster state can therefore select the
   mana arm; that arm requires unsigned mana `> 6` and costs 3 (`0x99367..0x993DD`). Otherwise,
   ammunition must exceed 1 and costs 1 (`0x993DF..0x9941A`).
3. CP 1.60 replaces the first transfer with a downward loop and a register-only running total.
   Its apparent defender-alive loop at `0x99367..0x9937F` is dead: both the hasted edge at
   `0x99362` and the no-Haste jump at `0x99364` bypass it. The repeat mana arm admits ranged types
   30–39 and either `Hero_Slot >= 0` or caster flags, requires unsigned mana `>= 6`, and costs 3.
4. CoM NOPs the quotient condition and makes the following ammo jump unconditional, so the mana
   arm is unreachable. Its dead arm would require unsigned mana `> 10` and cost 5; its reachable
   repeat consumes one ammo. These are control-flow facts, not intended-behavior guesses.
5. Defender gaze is resolved before wall-of-fire and first strike. Only gaze damage is accumulated
   in `Source_Unit_Damage`; later defender melee-counter bucket writes do not alter that value.
6. MoM 1.31 first-strike figure loss sums three separately truncated bucket quotients and omits
   attacker front-figure damage. CP and CoM use one quotient over the accumulated damage plus that
   front damage. The main and repeated attacker melee figure counts continue to ignore the
   defender's newly produced melee-counter buckets, preserving the routine's simultaneous/frozen
   figure-count behavior (`0x996D2..0x9972A`, `0x99750..0x9999D`).
7. MoM and CP repeat a hasted defender counter (`0x9985D..0x9989A`); CoM jumps directly over both
   that repeat and the R6.2d island. All builds retain the attacker's own Haste repeat
   (`0x9995F..0x999C1`).
8. The first-strike and ordinary-melee Cause Fear argument order is reversed in MoM 1.31 and
   exchanged in CP/CoM (`0x996CA`, `0x998D6`). The counter-site ordering at `0x997FB` is unchanged.

## Call-site admission

`BU_AttackTarget` has exactly **one** call site in every build. A full-file scan of both direct
call encodings — near `E8 rel16` and far `9A off:seg`, the latter resolved through the VROOMM
tables — finds a single near call at `0x9AF40` in `mom131`, `mom160` and `com1` alike. The call is
not reachable through any far-call table entry. Indirect calls through a function pointer would
not appear in such a scan and were not searched for.

That call is **unconditional**. It sits at the tail of the routine entered at `0x9AD04`, whose
last block computes the ranged-versus-melee *mode argument*, not an admission test:

```text
09AE38  mov al, es:[bx + 1]      ; attacker .ranged
09AE4B  mov al, es:[bx]          ; attacker .melee
09AE50  sub ax, dx / sar ax, 1   ; melee / 2
09AE55  cmp dx, ax               ; ranged > melee/2 ?
09AE57  jle 0x9AED6              ;   -> mode flag 0 (melee)
09AE5C  call 0x9BB03             ; Has_Ranged_Attack (0 < ranged_type < 100)
```

Every branch in that block — `0x9AE23`, `0x9AE28`, `0x9AEC8`, `0x9AECF`, `0x9AED6` — converges on
`0x9AEDB`, then `0x9AEE6`, then the call; no jump in the routine targets an address past
`0x9AF43`. The melee strength read at `0x9AE4B` therefore only chooses the attack mode. CoM 1's
bytes across this block and the call are identical to MoM 1.31's.

Consequence for the melee-path thrown/breath rider at `0x99499`: nothing upstream of
`BU_AttackTarget` withholds the call from a zero-melee attacker, so the rider's own
`ranged_type >= RAT_THROWN` test is the only gate on it. The routine at `0x9AD04` is itself
outside R6.2a's extent and is not reconstructed here; this establishes the admission behavior of
the call site, not that routine's semantics.
